Description & Eligibility
Teams will create a digital game based on the provided topic to entertain and educate. Teams may use
Scratch®, Tynker ®, or other game engines to create the executable game. Any middle level contestant may
enter this judged team event. A team will consist of 2 -4 contestants. Teams must be registered for the national
competition before the submission deadline and compete in both parts of the event to be ranked. Entries that
do not follow the provided topic , the Copyright and /or Fair Use Guidelines or Artificial Intelligence Usage &
Citation Policy will be disqualified.

Topic :
Embark on a journey to craft an educational game that explores the world of personal finance and
money management.

Task:
Design a game that teaches players how to budget, save, invest, and make smart financial decisions as they
navigate real-world scenarios — from landing their first job to handling unexpected expenses. Players should
face at least four pivotal financial dec isions that impact their in-game wealth and future outcomes.

The world you build should reflect realistic financial situations appropriate for a school setting. Will your
players retire early, fall into debt, or find the perfect balance? The outcome depends on the choices they
make.
