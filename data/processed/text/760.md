Topic
Entrepreneurship is often portrayed as a fast track to independence and innovation, fueled by social
media, online marketplaces, and digital tools. Starting a business has become more common and more
visible than ever with the help of social media, online marketplaces, and digital tools to sell products, offer
services, and promote ideas. Many young people see entrepreneurship as a way to earn income, be
creative, and work independently.

However, owning a business also comes with challenges. Entrepreneurs must take financial risks,
understand their market, follow laws and regulations, and handle uncertainty. Many new businesses
struggle or fail because of high costs, competition, or poor p lanning.

Your challenge: Create a professional presentation that explores what entrepreneurship really looks like
today. Use research from reliable sources along with real -world examples or personal observations to
explain both the benefits and the challenges of st arting and running a business. Highlight why people
choose to become entrepreneurs, the risks/challenges entrepreneurs face, how technology affects
entrepreneurship, and identify skills/traits needed to be a successful entrepreneur.

Finish your presentation by sharing three practical tips for contestants who are interested in starting a
business in the future.

Contest Competencies

1.
Create a clear, engaging multimedia presentation using modern digital tools to enhance key ideas

2. Use charts, graphs, and other visuals correctly and clearly to present information and support key
ideas
3. Deliver a professional, well -organized oral presentation using effective verbal and nonverbal
communication skills
4. Use credible research, facts, and supporting data to strengthen key ideas within the presentation

5. Analyze the assigned topic and organize a clear message using an effective introduction, body, and
conclusion
6. Prepare and submit a properly formatted Works Cited page that follows BPA’s
Style & Reference
Manual
7. Respond to judge questions clearly and confidently, using examples or reasoning to support your
answers

Contest Specifications for this Event

• The contestant is to make effective use of current multimedia technology in the presentation

• Contestant should use space, color, and text as design factors

• The use of graphics, including charts, should be a part of the presentation

• Format Works Cited according to the
Style & Reference Manual

Common Specifications for all BPA Judged Events

• The contestant is responsible for securing a signed BPA Release Form(s) from any person,
including oneself, whose image or work is used in the project.
• The contestant is responsible for citing all sources, including oneself, on the Works Cited page for
any work used on the project. It is the policy of Business Professionals of America to comply with
copyright law. The Style & Reference Manual contains guidelines for Copyright and/or Fair Use.
Contestants will be disqualified for violations of the guidelines.
