Description & Eligibility
Contestants will demonstrate foundational knowledge of security management across Windows® and
Linux® networking environments. This event focuses on essential skills in identifying threats, securing
systems, and applying core cybersecurity principles. Any secondary or postsecondary division contestant
may enter this objective event.

Contest Competencies
1.
Identify, assess, and mitigate security threats by deploying layered security tactics and managing
inbound connections to prevent, detect, and respond to attacks
2. Develop, implement, and manage infrastructure security controls and organizational security
policies to safeguard networks, systems, and data while ensuring compliance
3. Apply cryptography, encryption, authentication, and certificate management techniques
while monitoring security infrastructure to protect data and detect incidents
4. Harden Windows®, Linux, and network devices by applying security best practices, updates, and
configuration controls to reduce vulnerabilities and strengthen network defense
5. Secure and monitor TCP/IP and wireless networks by implementing encryption, authentication,
and traffic analysis to prevent unauthorized access
