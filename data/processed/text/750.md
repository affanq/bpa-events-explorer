750 Ethics and Professionalism

Description & Eligibility
Contestants will analyze and apply ethical frameworks to real
-world business situations. Contestants will
evaluate a scenario involving ethical dilemmas in areas such as leadership, technology, finance, marketing, or
human resources. Using critical -thinking and decision -making models, participants will present solutions
demonstrating integrity, professionalism, and alignment with organizational values. Evaluation will focus on
ethical reasoning, professionalism, communication, and problem -solving. Any secon dary or postsecondary
division contestant may enter this judged event.

Contestant Must Supply
• Ethics and Professionalism Resources Manual
• Sharpened pencils, pens

Equipment/Supplies Provided

• Three (3) notecards per contestant

Contest Notes
• Method of evaluation: judged
• AI Policy Level 1 No AI Allowed
• Length of event: no more than twenty (20) minutes preparation time, no less than five (5) minutes and
no more than seven (7) minutes for oral presentation, no more than three (3) minutes judges’ questions
• Finals may be part of the competition
• Each state is allowed three (3) entries

Contest Competencies

1.
Communicate ethical ideas clearly and professionally using effective verbal and nonverbal
communication skills
2. Organize the presentation using a clear introduction, body, and conclusion to explain and support an
ethical position
3. Apply recognized ethical principles and professional standards when evaluating dilemmas and making
decisions
4. Analyze ethical dilemmas and apply critical thinking to propose fair, logical, and defensible solutions.

5. Demonstrate integrity, professionalism, and respect when presenting ethical viewpoints and
interacting with others
6. Support arguments with relevant examples, evidence, policies, or real
-world business practices
7. Respond to judge questions clearly and confidently, using BPA’s Ethics and Professionalism Resources
Manual, scenario details, examples, and sound ethical reasoning developed during your preparation

Contest Specifications for this Event

• The contestant will be given a scenario dealing with ethics or professionalism. Refer to the
Ethics and
Professionalism Resources Manual as a guide when preparing for the event.
• The event proctor will provide three (3) notecards. Notes can only be made on the notecards
provided. Only the Ethics and Professionalism Resources Manual , along with three (3) notecards for
note taking, may be used in the preparation room.
• No personal electronic devices (smart watches, smart glasses, cell phone, etc.)

• No advisor contact will be allowed between the time of receiving the topic and the delivery

• Only the three (3) notecards may be used in the presentation room.
The Ethics and Professionalism
Resources Manual may not be used during the presentation.
• The contestant will be given warnings via flash cards when there are two (2) minutes remaining and
when there is one (1) minute remaining during the speaking time
• Judges’ comments will be returned digitally through the online judging system at the national level
