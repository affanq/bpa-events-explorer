Description & Eligibility
Contestants will create a letter requesting a job shadow experience and then be interviewed about what
careers they are interested in. Any middle level contestant may enter this judged team event. This event may
be repeated. Contestants must be registered for the national competition before the submission deadline and
compete in both parts of the event to be ranked.

Competitors Must Supply
• One (1) copy of the job shadow request letter formatted according to the
Style & Reference Manual
for each round of the event (preliminary and final)

Contest Notes
• Method of evaluation: judged
• AI Policy Level 1 No AI Allowed
• Length of event : no more than ten (10) minutes for interview
• Finals may be a part of the competition
• Each state is allowed three (3) entries
