Description & Eligibility
Contestants will apply accounting principles to analyze transactions, prepare
accurate financial
statements, and interpret data to support organizational decision -making. This application event is limited
to secondary division contestants who have not comp leted their first year of accounting. Contestants may
not enter Fundamental Accounting and Advanced Accounting in the same year. This event may
not be
repeated.

Contest Competencies

1.
Apply the steps of the accounting cycle to accurately process financial transactions and explain
their relevance in maintaining organizational financial integrity
2. Apply the fundamental accounting equation to analyze financial transactions and ensure accuracy
in reporting
3. Apply accounting standards to evaluate and report the value of assets, liabilities, and owner’s
equity in accordance with GAAP
4. Explain key accounting concepts and articulate the role of accounting systems in supporting
financial decision-making and organizational accountability
5. Analyze accruals and deferrals to ensure accurate financial reporting and alignment with
accounting principles and timing requirements
6. Prepare and interpret financial statements and reports to assess organizational performance and
support strategic decision -making
7. Apply adjusting and closing entries to update accounts and ensure
accurate period-end financial
reporting
8. Evaluate various forms of business ownership to determine their legal, financial, and operational
implications for organizational decision -making
9. Evaluate the characteristics and operational models of service and merchandising businesses to
guide financial practices and strategic decision -making
10. Analyze purchase and sales transactions for merchandise to ensure
accurate recording and
inventory management
