Description & Eligibility
Contestants will apply economic principles to analyze global and domestic trends, conduct research using
credible sources, and present evidence -based solutions in both a paper and a presentation. Contestants
must be registered for the national competition before the submission deadline and compete in both parts
of the event to be ranked. Entries that do not follow the provided topic or the Copyright and/or Fair Use
Guidelines and Artificial Intelligence Usage & Citation Policy
will be disqualified.

Topic
The Gig Economy and Income Stability . How has the rise of gig work (Uber, DoorDash, freelance platforms)
reshaped traditional employment models, and what are the long -term economic implications for workers
who forgo traditional benefits, pensions, and job security in favor of flexible, indepen
dent income
streams?

Considerations
• Wage volatility and income unpredictability for gig workers
• Lack of employer -sponsored benefits (health insurance, retirement, paid leave)
• Tax implications and financial planning challenges for independent contractors

• Labor market shifts and the decline of traditional full -time employment
• Circular flow implications

equipment and external displays will not be provided or permitted.

• Length of event: no more than three (3) minutes setup time, no more than seven (7) minutes

Contest Competencies

1.
Apply foundational economic concepts to analyze and interpret global and domestic economic
trends
2. Conduct rigorous economic research using credible sources and communicate findings clearly and
professionally through oral presentations and written reports
3. Analyze workplace economic factors such as productivity, labor markets, and technological change
to inform organizational strategy and decision -making
4. Compare and contrast global economic systems, evaluating their effectiveness in addressing
contemporary economic challenges
5. Evaluate strategies for promoting sustainable economic growth and apply modern metrics to
measure economic performance
6. Utilize diverse research methodologies and digital resources to gather, analyze, and interpret
economic data, ensuring ethical standards and data integrity
7. Critically analyze economic issues using quantitative and qualitative methods and propose
evidence-based solutions aligned with industry trends
