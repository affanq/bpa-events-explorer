Topic - NextGen Athlete — Building the Brand of a Rising Sports Star

NextGen Athlete is a fictitious sports marketing agency representing a talented young professional athlete
who recently gained national attention following a breakout season. While the athlete’s on-field
performance has generated excitement among fans, their personal brand and media presence are still
developing.

The agency believes there is an opportunity to build a recognizable and marketable athlete brand through
a strategic sports media marketing campaign that leverages social media platforms, digital storytelling, and
partnerships with sponsors and influencers . A strong personal brand can help increase fan engagement,
expand the athlete’s audience, and attract endorsement opportunities. The athlete’s current fan base
primarily consists of younger sports fans who actively follow athletes through platforms such a s Instagram,
TikTok, YouTube, and other digital media channels. However, the athlete faces competition from many
other rising athletes who are also building their presence across sports media platforms.

Your consulting firm has been hired to develop a sports media marketing campaign designed to strengthen
the athlete’s personal brand and increase fan engagement. Your campaign should demonstrate how sports
media marketing strategies can build brand loyalty, expand the athlete’s audience, and create
opportunities for partnerships and sponsorships. Teams should analyze the target audience, evaluate the
competitive sports media landscape, and design an integrated marketing campaign that leverages digital
media, social platforms, and emerging technologies. Teams may make reasonable assumptions where data
is not provided; however, assumptions should be reasonable, clearly explained, and supported by research
where possible. Teams must develop their campaign based on the NextGen Athlete concept described in
this case study and may create a fictional athlete profile consistent with the NextGen Athlete scenario.
Campaigns proposing unrelated organizations or athletes will not be evaluated.

Teams should consider elements such as:
• target audience demographics and fan engagement behavior
• athlete brand identity and positioning
• social media marketing strategies and content creation
• influencer collaborations and partnerships
• sponsorship and endorsement opportunities
• integrated digital media promotion
• creative campaign elements such as short -form video or teaser content
• long-term brand growth and fan loyalty
