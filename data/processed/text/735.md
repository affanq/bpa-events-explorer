Description & Eligibility
Contestants will demonstrate professional and persuasive oral communication by organizing and delivering
a well-structured, extemporaneous speech on a business -related topic within a limited preparation period.
Contestants will analyze the assigned prompt, develop key ideas with relevant supporting examples, and
present a coherent message that reflects logical structure, confident delivery, and understanding of
business concepts. Evaluation will focus on organization, audience awareness, delivery techniques
, and the
ethical use of language. Any secondary division contestant may enter this judged event.

Contestant Must Supply
• Sharpened pencils, pens

Equipment/Supplies Provided

• Three (3) notecards per contestant
• Topics

Contest Notes
• Method of evaluation: judged
• AI Policy Level 1 No AI Allowed
• Length of event: no more than ten (10) minutes preparation, no less than two (2) and no more than
four (4) minutes speech
• The contestant will be given warnings via flash cards when there are two (2) minutes remaining and
when there is one (1) minute remaining during the speaking time
• Finals may be part of the competition
• Each state is allowed three (3) entries

Contest Competencies

1.
Demonstrate clear, professional, and effective oral communication when presenting ideas

2. Organize the speech using a clear introduction, body, and conclusion to create a coherent
message
3. Use appropriate tempo, pitch, and vocal variety to enhance clarity and audience engagement

4. Use purposeful nonverbal gestures, posture, and eye contact to support confident delivery

5. Analyze the assigned topic quickly and develop key points supported by logical reasoning,
examples, and relevant business insights
6. Adapt tone, language, and delivery to meet audience expectations
7. Use effective opening and closing statements to strengthen the overall message

8. Demonstrate professionalism, confidence, and ethical communication while presenting under
pressure

Contest Specifications for this Event

• Contestant will draw two (2) different business topics and will select one. The topics may deal with
Business Professionals of America, office situations, the business world, etc.

• The event proctor will provide three (3) notecards. Notes can only be made on the notecards
provided
• No advisor contact will be allowed between the time of receiving the topic and the delivery

• No materials or previously prepared notes will be allowed into the preparation or presentation
room with the contestant
• No personal electronic devices (smart watches, smart glasses, cell phone, etc.)

• Judges’ comments will be returned digitally through the online judging system at the national level
