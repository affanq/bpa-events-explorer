110 Advanced Accounting

Description & Eligibility
Contestants will execute the accounting cycle for various business structures, apply GAAP, and prepare
and analyze financial statements to ensure accuracy, compliance, and data -driven decision -making. Any
secondary division contestant may enter this applic ation event. Contestants may not enter Fundamental
Accounting and Advanced Accounting in the same year.

Contestant Must Supply
• Published and/or unpublished non -electronic written reference materials
• Sharpened pencils, pens, ruler
• Cordless calculator: Electronic devices will be monitored according to ACT standards.  See NLC
Calculator Guidelines.  Contestants who violate this rule will be
disqualified.

Equipment/Supplies Provided

• Computer and software

Competition Notes
• Method of evaluation: objective | application
• AI Policy Level 1 No AI Allowed
• No equipment, supplies, or materials other than those specified for an event will be allowed in the
testing area
• Previous Business Professionals of America tests and/or sample tests or facsimiles (handwritten,
photocopied or keyed) may not be taken into the testing area. Violation of this rule will result in
disqualification.
• Length of event: no more than ten (10) minutes for orientation, no more than ninety (90) minutes
for testing, and no more than ten (10) minutes for wrap -up
• Entries vary by state

Contest Competencies

1.
Execute and analyze the accounting cycle using accounting systems and automation tools to
ensure accuracy, compliance, and efficiency
2. Apply GAAP and relevant international standards to assess and report the value of assets,
liabilities, and equity
3. Apply accounting principles across service, merchandising, and
various business structures
4. Analyze and record business and equity transactions using integrated accounting platforms,
emphasizing automation, audit trails, and compliance
5. Evaluate and manage uncollectible accounts using predictive analytics within accounting platforms

6. Apply depreciation methods within accounting systems, incorporating tax regulations and asset
lifecycle management
7. Calculate and analyze interest using financial modeling tools, with emphasis on real
-time data and
compliance
8. Apply inventory valuation methods using inventory management systems integrated with
accounting platforms
9. Prepare, interpret, and analyze financial statements, emphasizing real-time data to support
decision-making
10. Perform departmental allocations using integrated financial systems, focusing on cost centers,
shared services, and performance metrics
