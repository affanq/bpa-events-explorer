## Page 1

720 Advanced Workplace Skills

(formerly 225 Advanced Office Systems and Procedures)

Description & Eligibility
Contestants will act as an Office Specialist for Digital Solutions and use given materials to produce
accurate business documents that reflect workplace expectations. Any secondary or postsecondary
division contestant may enter this application event. This event may be repeated.

Contestant Must Supply
• Published and/or unpublished non -electronic written reference materials
• Cordless calculator: Electronic devices will be monitored according to ACT standards. See
NLC
Calculator Guidelines . Contestants who violate this rule will be disqualified.
• Suggested resource: ANSI/ARMA 1 -1995 - Alphabetic Filing Rules

Equipment/Supplies Provided

• Computer and software

Contest Notes
• Method of evaluation: objective | application
• AI Policy Level 1 No AI Allowed
• All contestant-produced jobs completed on -site are uploaded electronically and are not printed at
the national level
• Previous Business Professionals of America tests and/or sample tests (handwritten, photocopied
or keyed) may not be taken into the testing area. Violation of this rule will result in disqualification.

• Length of event: no more than ten (10) minutes orientation, no more than ninety (90) minutes
testing time, and no more than ten (10) minutes wrap -up
• Entries vary by state

Contest Competencies

1. Demonstrate proficiency in technology and information management by using industry
-standard
software to create, edit, organize, and format business documents
2. Interpret and analyze provided information to determine appropriate document type, structure,
and content, ensuring accuracy
3. Apply effective written communication and professionalism by producing clear, well
-formatted
documents that follow correct grammar, tone, organizational guidelines, and ethical standards
4. Exhibit employability and workplace skills, including time management, attention to detail,
reliability, and safe, responsible use of office tools and equipment
5. Organize and maintain office inventory and filing systems using digital tools and ARMA standards
