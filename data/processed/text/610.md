610 Health Administration Procedures

Description & Eligibility
Contestants will demonstrate proficiency in essential medical office administrative tasks, including
managing documents, communicating with patients, processing payments, controlling inventory, and
maintaining compliant electronic health records. Any secon dary or postsecondary division contestant may
enter this application event.

Contestant Must Supply
• Published and/or unpublished non -electronic written reference materials
• (Optional) Cordless calculator: Electronic devices will be monitored according to ACT standards.
See NLC Calculator Guidelines. Contestants who violate this rule will be disqualified.

Contest Competencies

1.
Create, manage, and generate medical office documents and reports

2. Demonstrate proficiency in medical office workflows and ethical standards for both in
-person and
virtual interactions, ensuring compliance with HIPAA standards
3. Accurately process patient payments and maintain organized financial records in compliance with
office procedures
4. Demonstrate knowledge of the medical office terminology and office procedures

5. Prepare and maintain inventory of equipment and supplies
6. Provide patient-centered support through secure digital communication channels, telehealth
platforms, and integrated EHR messaging systems and voicemail transcription while maintaining
HIPAA compliance
7. Organize and maintain electronic health records and digital filing systems in compliance with
ARMA and HIPAA standards
