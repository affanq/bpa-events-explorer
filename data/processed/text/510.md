Description & Eligibility
Teams will research, develop, and deliver an investor -ready pitch for a business idea. Modeled after a real
-
world investor pitch, teams will present their concept to a panel of judges acting as potential investors,
covering the business opportunity, target market, competitive advantage, financial projections, and growth
strategy. Any secondary or postsecondary division contestant may enter this judged team event. A team
will consist of 2 -4 contestants. Entries that do not follow the provided topic, or the C opyright and/or Fair
Use Guidelines and the Artificial Intelligence Usage & Citation Policy will be disqualified.

Topic

PulsePeak Events is a fictitious startup entertainment company planning to launch a two -day outdoor
music festival designed to attract Gen Z and young millennial audiences. The event will feature emerging
artists, regional performers, local food vendors, and interactive f
an experiences intended to generate
strong social media engagement.

The founders believe there is an opportunity to build a festival brand focused on music discovery,
shareable experiences, and strong digital promotion. If successful,
PulsePeak Events could expand to
additional cities in future years. PulsePeak Events is planned for a mid -sized metropolitan area with a
strong youth and college population and an active live entertainment scene. Revenue may come from
several sources including ticket sales, sponsorships, vendor fees, merchandise, and VIP experiences.
However, launching a new festival presents challenges such as attracting attendees in its first year,
securing sponsors, managing event costs, and competing with established entertainment events.

Your consulting firm has been hired to develop and present an investor
-ready pitch for PulsePeak Events .
Your pitch should explain the business opportunity, target market, competitive landscape and
PulsePeak
Events’ competitive advantage, marketing strategy, revenue model, estimated startup costs, and financial
projections.
Teams should present their concept as if they are seeking investment funding for the proposed venture.

Teams may make reasonable assumptions where data is not provided; however, assumptions should be
reasonable, clearly explained, and supported by research where possible.

Teams must develop their pitch based on the PulsePeak Events business concept described in this case
study. Presentations proposing an unrelated business idea will not be evaluated. The focus of the
presentation should be the business opportunity and investment potential of
PulsePeak Events rather
than only the event logistics.

Teams should consider elements such as:
• market demand
• pricing strategy
• sponsorship opportunities
• marketing strategy
• long-term growth potential

Team Must Supply

Style & Reference Manual, signed
BPA Release Form(s ), and AI Usage and Documentation Form for each round of the event

• Students may use one single -sided laptop, notebook, or tablet for their presentation. Projection

Contest Notes
• Method of evaluation: judged
• AI Policy Level 2 Permitted with Citation
• Carry-in and setup of equipment must be done solely by the team and must take place within the
time allotted
• No Internet access will be provided on -site at NLC; however, teams may provide their own access
to be used only for their presentation to the judges
• Length of event: no more than three (3) minutes setup, no less than seven (7) and no more than ten
(10) minutes oral presentation, no more than five (5) minutes judges’ questions
• Finals may be part of the competition
• Each state is allowed three (3) entries

Contest Competencies

1.
Analyze an assigned business idea and develop a clear, compelling value proposition that
communicates the opportunity to potential investors
2. Identify and analyze the target market, including customer demographics, needs, and market size

3. Evaluate the competitive landscape and articulate the business's unique competitive advantage

4. Develop and justify basic financial projections including startup costs, revenue potential, and
profitability
5. Create and present a realistic go -to-market and growth strategy for the proposed business
6. Deliver a persuasive, professional investor pitch using effective verbal and nonverbal
communication skills
7. Respond confidently and knowledgeably to judges' questions, demonstrating deep understanding
of the business concept
8. Collaborate effectively as a team by assigning roles and delivering a cohesive, well
-organized
presentation
