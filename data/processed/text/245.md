Description & Eligibility
Teams will analyze a real -world small business case and develop data -driven, strategic solutions to address
identified challenges. Using critical thinking and collaborative problem

present an action plan with recommen dations addressing financial, operational, human resource, and
marketing impacts. At the state and national levels, teams will receive an additional scenario element
requiring rapid analysis and revision of their strategy before final presentation.
Any secondary or
postsecondary division contestant may enter this judged team event. A team will consist of 2

compete in both parts of the event to be ranked. Entries that do not follow the provided topic or the
Copyright and/or Fair Use Guidelines and the Artificial Intelligence Usage & Citation Policy will be
disqualified.

Topic
SmartNest Dynamics, founded by Evan Harper, is a smart -home installation and support company that has
grown steadily over the past three years. The team installs smart lighting, climate control, security systems,
entertainment setups, and whole -home automation for h omes and small offices in a fast -growing suburban
metro area. Known for reliable, high -quality service,  SmartNest Dynamics is typically booked 2 –3 weeks
out, and subscription services now make up 40% of revenue. The company currently has six installers and
two support staff.

Even with strong demand, Evan is reaching a turning point.  SmartNest Dynamics is busier than ever and
keeping up is difficult. Heavy installer workloads, rising support requests, and Evan’s responsibilities with
scheduling, communication, training, and inventory make planning ahead challenging. Meanwhile, new
opportunities—smart-office installations, AI -powered features, and subscription growth —are emerging, but
current systems and staffing may not fully support a large expansion.

Evan hopes to grow  SmartNest Dynamics sustainably—improving operations, exploring  new
technology options, and  maintaining the company’s  strong reputation. He has hired your team to
recommend a strategic growth plan for the coming year.

Your plan should include:
• Key actions SmartNest Dynamics should take to support sustainable growth
• Recommendations to improve staffing, workflow, or daily operations
• A decision on whether  SmartNest Dynamics should begin offering AI -based smart-home features this
year
• Ways to strengthen customer satisfaction and overall service quality

Considerations:
• Which growth opportunity  SmartNest Dynamics should prioritize first
• How day-to-day operations can be streamlined as demand increases
• What factors SmartNest Dynamics should evaluate before introducing AI
-powered features

Contest Competencies

1.
Develop and implement a strategic action plan, effectively managing time, finances, and personnel
to achieve measurable outcomes
2. Apply entrepreneurial, managerial, and problem -solving principles to address business challenges
and develop innovative solutions
3. Interpret and communicate data -driven insights, identifying key issues, assumptions, and evidence
to support business recommendations
4. Conduct financial and situational analyses using SWOT, forecasting, and other strategic tools to
evaluate short- and long-term impacts
5. Analyze team strengths, define roles, and delegate responsibilities to meet project goals

6. Deliver a persuasive, data -supported team presentation using clear visuals and appropriate digital
tools
7. Apply ethical, legal, and socially responsible decision -making in all business recommendations
8. Use digital collaboration and productivity tools to plan, analyze, and present business solutions

9. Evaluate business strategies using KPIs and analytics to measure performance and guide decisions

10. Use innovation and change -management principles to create and implement effective business
solutions
