## Page 1

700 Fundamental Word Processing

(formerly 200)

Description & Eligibility
Contestants will demonstrate entry -level word processing skills by creating, editing and formatting
business documents. Only secondary or postsecondary division contestants who have completed one year
(or less) of word processing and are not enrolled in the second year may enter this application event.
Contestants who have previously competed in Fundamental, Intermediate, or Advanced Word Processing
cannot enter this event. Contestants may participate in only one of these events each year.

Contestant Must Supply
• Published and/or unpublished non -electronic written reference materials

Equipment/Supplies Provided

• Computer and software

Contest Notes
• Method of evaluation: application
• AI Policy Level 1 No AI Allowed
• All contestant-produced jobs completed on -site are uploaded electronically and are not printed at
the national level
• Previous Business Professionals of America tests and/or sample tests (handwritten, photocopied
or keyed) may not be taken into the testing area. Violation of this rule will result in disqualification.

• Length of event: no more than ten (10) minutes orientation, no more than sixty (60) minutes testing
time, and no more than ten (10) minutes wrap-up
• At NLC, contestants will take the Word Processing (Precision Exams by YouScience) certification
with their contest. Contestants passing this certification will have 50 points added to their event
score. Reference materials are not allowed for certification testing. For more information, visit
https://certifications.youscience.com/exams/
• Entries vary by state

Contest Competencies

1.
Apply foundational formatting skills using modern word processing tools to create business
documents
2. Demonstrate entry -level word processing using standard word processing tools

3. Use editing tools to revise, proofread, and enhance the clarity and accuracy of business
documents
4. Apply paragraph formatting, tab settings and text enhancements to improve document structure
and visual impact
