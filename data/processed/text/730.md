Description & Eligibility
Contestants will assess career readiness and professional communication through the job search,
interview, and portfolio presentation process. Contestants will prepare a resume, cover letter, and
professional portfolio demonstrating their skills, experienc es, and accomplishments. Participants will
research career qualifications, present a professional image, and participate in an advanced interview
scenario in an in -person format. Evaluation will emphasize professionalism, portfolio quality,
communication effectiveness, and understanding of ethical workplace practices.
Any secondary or
postsecondary division contestant may enter this judged event. Contestants may
not enter both Interview
Skills and Advanced Interview Skills in the same year. Advanced Interview Skills may be repeated.
Contestants must be registered for the national competition before the submission deadline and compete
in both parts of the event to be ranked.

Contest Competencies

1.
Create a tailored Applicant Tracking System (ATS) optimized résumé, cover letter, and a
professional portfolio showcasing skills, accomplishments, and qualifications
2. Demonstrate strong employability and career advancement skills including adaptability, initiative,
and continuous learning
3. Research career opportunities, industry trends, credentials, and expectations for professional
image and branding
4. Communicate respectfully and ethically in diverse work environments, demonstrating integrity and
effective collaboration with others
5. Exhibit advanced interview communication skills through persuasive storytelling, situational
awareness, and confident interpersonal interaction
6. Demonstrate professionalism when discussing salary, benefits, and job offers

7. Use networking skills to build professional connections through career events, organizations, and
personal interactions
8. Demonstrate advanced interviewing skills by giving clear, confident answers to interview and judge
questions, and using examples to explain skills and experiences

• Business cards, thank you notes, etc. are not permitted
