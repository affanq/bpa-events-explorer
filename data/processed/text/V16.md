V16 Virtual Artificial Intelligence Team
Description & Eligibility
Teams will design and present an integrated artificial intelligence (AI) solution for a subscription
-based
SaaS company using the official BPA case study and data pack. Teams will use generative and analytical AI
tools to interpret the data and produce pro fessional artifacts across four functional areas: Finance,
Information Technology (IT), Business/Marketing, and Digital Media Design. This national event will be
submitted and judged virtually. Any middle level, secondary, or postsecondary contestant may e
nter this
event. A team will consist of 2 –4 contestants. There are no restrictions on the number of entries per
chapter or per state. Teams who do not submit an entry following the topic listed below or do not follow
the Copyright and/or Fair Use Guideline s and the Artificial Intelligence Usage and Citation Policy will be
disqualified.

Topic
LoopToLearn — An AI -Assisted EdTech Subscription Platform. Your team has been hired as AI strategy
consultants for LoopToLearn , a five -year-old subscription -based SaaS company that provides AI
-assisted
tutoring and study-skills coaching for high school and early -college students. LoopToLearn offers three
subscription tiers (Student, Family, and School District) and is preparing for a Series B funding round. The
leadership team has provided your firm with an official BPA case study and data pack containing financial
records, IT infrastructure logs, customer/marketing data, and current brand assets. Your team must use AI
tools to analyze the data pack and produce four integrated deliverables that demonstrate ethical,
responsible, and reproducible AI use across Finance, IT, Business/Marketing, an
d Digital Media Design.

Deliverables
• Finance Artifact — A two-page decision brief incorporating a subscription revenue forecast, a churn
analysis, and a Series B funding recommendation (PDF, two pages maximum)

• IT Artifact — A two-page infrastructure and security brief incorporating a cloud
-scaling plan, a data -
privacy/FERPA risk assessment, and an AI model selection recommendation (PDF, two pages
maximum)
• Business/Marketing Artifact — A two-page brief incorporating a customer segmentation analysis and
a go-to-market campaign brief targeting the Family and School District tiers (PDF, two pages
maximum)
• Digital Media Design Artifact — AI-assisted brand refresh deliverable including a one -page visual
identity sheet (logo treatment, color, typography) and a 30 -second promotional video storyboard for
the campaign (PDF, with video link embedded)
• AI Use Log — A single combined document listing every AI tool used, the prompts issued, the
date/time of each interaction, and a short note on how the team verified and edited each output
(PDF, no page limit)

Page limit and format specifications: Artifacts 1, 2, and 3 are limited to two (2) pages each, maximum. The
two-page limit is inclusive of all charts, tables, and visuals; the Works Cited page is excluded from the
count. All artifact reports must follow th e report format defined in the Style & Reference Manual. The
Digital Media Design Artifact (Artifact 4) follows its own format described above and is not subject to the
two-page limit.
