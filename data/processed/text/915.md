915 Administrative Support Team
(Dedicated to the memory of Deborah Paul )

Description & Eligibility
Teams will function as office staff producing a variety of business documents. Any middle level contestant may
enter this application team event. A team will consist of 2 -4 contestants.

Contestant Must Supply
• Published and/or unpublished non -electronic written reference materials

Equipment/ Supplies Provided
• Computer (one per team contestant), software, printer, and paper
• Flash drive (one per team, which must be submitted with test materials at conclusion of testing)

Contest Notes
• Method of evaluation: application
• AI Policy Level 1 No AI Allowed
• All student-produced jobs completed on -site are uploaded electronically and are not printed at the
national level
• No equipment, supplies, or materials other than those specified for an event will be allowed in the
testing area
• Previous Business Professionals of America tests (handwritten, photocopied, or keyed) may not be
taken into the testing area. Violation of this rule will result in disqualification.
• Length of event : no more than ten (10) minutes orientation, no more than sixty (60) minutes testing
time, no more than ten (10) minutes wrap-up
• Entries vary by state
Contest Competencies
1.
Create, manage and format business documents through collaboration following specified formats
provided in the BPA Style & Reference Manual
2. Apply technical skills to manage information and produce business documents

3. Evaluate and delegate responsibilities needed to perform required tasks

4. Plan and manage projects by establishing work priorities and timelines
5. Demonstrate teamwork skills needed to function as an office staff

6. Manage digital and physical output, including exporting PDF files

7. Proofread and edit to ensure accuracy and consistency
