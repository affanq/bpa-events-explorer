690 Healthcare & Human Services Concepts (Open)
(formerly 690 Health Administration Concepts)

Description & Eligibility
Contestants will demonstrate general knowledge of medical front office procedures, including the
structure of the healthcare system, effective patient communication and scheduling, regulatory and ethical
standards, billing and insurance processes, and the proper management of patient records and office
documentation.  Any secondary or postsecondary division contestant may enter this objective event.

Contestant Must Supply
• (Optional) Cordless calculator: Electronic devices will be monitored according to ACT standards.
See NLC Calculator Guidelines . Contestants who violate this rule will be disqualified.

Competition Notes
• Method of evaluation: objective
• AI Policy Level 1 No AI Allowed
• Reference materials are not allowed. Contestants who violate this rule will be disqualified.
• Length of event: no more than ten (10) minutes orientation, no more than sixty (60) minutes testing
time, no more than ten (10) minutes wrap-up
• Unlimited entries by state

Contest Competencies

1.
Demonstrate understanding of medical office roles, healthcare delivery settings, and organizational
structures
2. Apply knowledge of effective patient interaction, appointment scheduling, and front office
workflow procedures
3. Understand and apply HIPAA, patient confidentiality, and ethical guidelines in medical office
operations
4. Demonstrate knowledge of insurance verification, claims processing, payment collection, and
general revenue cycle concepts
5. Apply proper procedures for organizing, maintaining, and safeguarding patient records and office
documentation
