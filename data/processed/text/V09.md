V09 Virtual Financial Portfolio Management Team

Description & Eligibility
Teams will enter an investment simulation using an online platform provided by a BPA partner. The simulation
will provide the students with $100,000 initially, to be used to invest in stocks, mutual funds, and/or bonds.
This national event will be submitte d and judged virtually. Any middle level, secondary or postsecondary
contestant may enter this event. A team will consist of 2 -4 contestants. There are no restrictions on the
number of entries per chapter or per state. Teams who do not submit an entry following the topic listed below
or do not follow the Copyright and/or Fair Use Guidelines and Artificial Intelligence Usage and Citation Policy
will be disqualified.

Topic (Top 20 contestants from Round 1 only)

You have just accepted a job as a financial advisor with a major brokerage firm. On your first day at work, a 19
-
year-old walks in and says he just inherited $100,000. He just started college and needs to invest $60,000
conservatively over the next 4 years so he can pay for college, but he wants to make as much money as he can
on the other $40k over the next 5 years so he can buy a car and maybe a down payment on a house a year or
two after graduates from college. What will you suggest to him?

The initial $100,000 MUST be used to purchase the following:

• Students must purchase at least $8,000 -$10,000 of shares in at least 5 different stocks each in a
different industry (i.e., diversify!) ....by 3:59 pm ET on Friday, December 19, 2026, and hold at least 5 of
these original stocks for the entire time of the stock market simulation
• Two of these five stocks must have a dividend yield greater than 3%

• Teams MUST purchase and hold $10,000 in mutual funds by 3:59 pm ET on Friday, December 19, 2026,
and hold at least $10,000 in a mutual fund for the entire time of the stock market simulation

o Teams who advance to round two presentations should be prepared to explain why they
picked the stocks that they did
• Teams may use the remaining $40,000 to purchase (no shorting) additional stocks, ETFs or mutual funds.

• All stocks must have a minimum price of $10 per share
• Teams may buy and sell these stocks as they wish, with each transaction subject to a $10 commission
fee which will be subtracted their cash balance
• Teams can buy any investment allowed through the NYSE and NASDAQ markets, including stocks,
mutual funds, and bonds
• Teams will be limited on any one investment to 10% of their portfolio
• Teams will be allowed to buy on margin up to 100% of their cash balance. Any loan will be charged 8%
interest
• Teams will receive 3% interest on any cash balance maintained
• Teams MUST make a minimum of six purchases by December 19th to be eligible for placement.

• If you qualify for a presentation, you must clearly state the 5 stocks and 1 mutual fund that you
purchased and held, the performance and industry of each, and why you chose those 6 securities

Contest Competencies

1.
Research and analyze various investment options, evaluating cash and asset allocation strategies to
maximize return within given restrictions
2. Calculate investment amounts and return on investments, applying relevant formulas and financial
concepts to assess performance and value
3. Manage an online investment fund, applying real -world decision -making to monitor and adjust holdings
over time
4. Determine the timing of buying and selling investments by interpreting market trends, economic
indicators, and relevant financial factors
5. Prepare investment reports using spreadsheet software to organize, calculate, and present financial
data in a clear and professional format
6. Develop and deliver a presentation of investment strategies, communicating findings, rationale, and
recommendations to a professional audience

Contest Specifications for this Event

• All registered teams will participate in a virtual stock market challenge. Tutorials and login credentials
will be emailed to registered teams by early December 2026.

• The top twenty (20) teams, in terms of portfolio value, will be invited to
design a computer-generated
multimedia presentation on their strategies for success and challenges faced.
The presentation should
make effective use of current multimedia technology (e.g., sound, movement, digital video, etc.).
• Advisors of qualifying teams will be contacted to reserve a time for a videoconference presentation
before a panel of judges. The dates and times for presentation reservations will be announced via
email notification. Teams will be assigned to sections pri or to the preliminary presentation.
• Length of event: no more than three (3) minutes for setup, no more than ten (10) minutes for the
presentation, and no more than five (5) minutes for judges’ questions.
• Teams with the highest cumulative presentation scores will be invited to attend the National
Leadership Conference to be recognized for their outstanding efforts and to participate in the
National Showcase.

Common Specifications for all BPA Judged Events

• Team ID is required for all submissions. Materials from non -registered contestants and/or those
missing Team ID cannot be accepted.
• The team is responsible for securing a BPA Release Form(s) from any person, including
themselves, whose image or work is used in the project.
• The team is responsible for citing all sources, including themselves, on the Works Cited page for
any work used on the project. It is the policy of Business Professionals of America to comply with

Contestants will be disqualified for violations of the guidelines.

logo and/or organization’s name. Refer to the BPA Graphic Standards in the Style & Reference
Manual.
• Judges’ comments will be returned digitally through the online judging system at the national level.
Materials submitted for technical judging will not be returned and will not be available at NLC.
