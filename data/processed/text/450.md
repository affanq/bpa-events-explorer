Description & Eligibility
The team will research and create a three to five (3:00 -5:00) minute podcast based on the assigned topic.
Any secondary or postsecondary division contestant may enter this judged team event. A team will consist
of 2-4 contestants. Teams must be registered for the national competition before the submission deadline
and compete in both parts of the event to be ranked. Entries that do not follow the provided topic or the
Copyright and/or Fair Use Guidelines and the Artificial Intelligence Usage and Citation Policy will be
disqualified.

Topic
Podcast Production Team – Myth vs. Reality: Teenagers and Technology . Create a three (3:00) to five
(5:00) minute podcast episode exploring common myths about teenagers’ use of technology and
comparing them to real experiences, facts, and perspectives.

Your podcast must include the following:
• Address at least two (2) widely believed myths about teenagers and technology
• Two required myths:
o
Myth #1: “Teenagers are addicted to their phones and cannot function without them.”
o
Myth #2: “Technology only distracts teens and harms their learning.”
• Contrast each myth with real -world evidence, examples, or lived experiences.
• Demonstrate effective audio production techniques (voice clarity, pacing, transitions, and sound
elements).
• Include a clear takeaway message about how technology truly affects teenagers today.

Teams may include additional myths but must cover both required myths. The podcast should be
informative, balanced, and appropriate for a K – 12 school audience.

equipment and external displays will not be provided or permitted .
