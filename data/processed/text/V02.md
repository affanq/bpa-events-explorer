V02 Virtual Multimedia and Promotion Team

Description & Eligibility
Teams will create a 3:00 to 5:00 minute promotional video based on the provided topic. This national
event will be submitted and judged virtually. Any middle level, secondary or postsecondary contestant may
enter this event. A team will consist of 2 -4 contestants. There are no restrictions on the number of entries
per chapter or per state. Teams who do not submit an entry following the topic listed below or do not
follow the Copyright and/or Fair Use Guidelines or Artificial Intelligence Usage & Citation Po
licy will be
disqualified.

Topic
Game Changers . Student Athletes Who Are Making a Difference Off the Field. Create a 3:00 to 5:00
minute compelling multimedia promotion that spotlights student athletes in your community who are using
their platform, time, and influence to give back — through mentorship, charity work, advocacy, or
community leadership. Choose a story with emotional depth and cultural relevance and use promotional
strategies to inspire other young people to lead with purpose beyond the game.

Contest Competencies

1.
Demonstrate proficiency in industry -standard video production software and equipment to execute
all phases of the production process
2. Develop a cohesive storyline using a storyboard and script that applies effective scripting
techniques to deliver a clear, focused message
3. Apply professional camera techniques, including intentional selection of shot types, angles, and
framing to support visual storytelling
4. Implement lighting techniques, including proper setup and adjustment of light sources, to achieve
consistent and professional visual quality
5. Perform non-linear editing using industry -standard tools, incorporating transitions, cuts, and audio
editing techniques to produce a seamless and polished final cut

6. Apply titles, lower thirds, and 2D and/or 3D visual effects to enhance the final product and

Contest Specifications for this Event

• The completed final product dimensions should be at least 1920 x 1080.
• Submit the URL to the Promotional Video , Works Cited, BPA Release Form(s), and AI Usage and
Documentation Form in a combined PDF file to https://upload.bpa.org no later than
11:59 p.m.
Eastern Time, on January 15, 2027.
• Advisors of qualifying teams will be contacted to reserve a time for a videoconference
presentation before a panel of judges. The dates and times for presentation reservations will be
announced via email notification. Teams will be assigned to sections pri
or to the preliminary
presentation.
• Length of event: no more than three (3) minutes for setup, no more than ten (10) minutes for the
presentation, and no more than five (5) minutes for judges’ questions.
• The top contestants with the highest cumulative technical and presentation scores will be invited
to attend the National Leadership Conference to be recognized for their outstanding efforts and
to participate in the National Showcase.

Common Specifications for all BPA judged events

-registered contestants

Reference Manual.

level. Materials submitted for technical judging will not be returned and will not be available at
NLC.
