V12 Virtual Social Media Marketing Campaign Team

Description & Eligibility
Teams will develop a social media marketing campaign, following the guidelines outlined in the
Style &
Reference Manual , that details pricing strategies and promotional plans for a business. This national event will
be submitted and judged virtually. Any middle level, secondary or postsecondary contestant may enter this
event. A team will consist of 2 -4 contestants. There are no restrictions on the number of entries per chapter or
per state. Teams who do not submit an entry following the topic listed below, do not follow the Copyright
and/or Fair Use Guidelines and the Artificial Intelligence Usage & Citation Policy will be
disqualified.

Topic
Game Day Every Day  — Building a Fan Community for a Minor League Sports Team.
The Riverside Rockets, a
fictitious minor league baseball team, is struggling with low attendance and limited community awareness in its
market. The team's new ownership group wants to completely reimagine their social media presence to drive
ticket sales, build a loyal local fan base, and attract sponsorships.

Task
Your consulting firm has been  retained to develop and present a comprehensive social media marketing
campaign for the Riverside Rockets. Your campaign must include a community engagement strategy, a content
calendar concept across at least two platforms, a sponsorship promotion plan, geo
-targeted advertising
recommendations, a detailed campaign budget with ROI estimates, and measurable success metrics tied to
ticket sales and attendance growth.

Contest Competencies

1.
Develop a comprehensive written social media marketing campaign that integrates strategy, content
planning, and measurable goals aligned to business objectives
2. Demonstrate effective oral and written communication skills, including the ability to present research,
information, and persuasive content in a clear and professional manner

3. Identify and utilize internal and external resources to support campaign planning, content
development, and marketing execution
4. Identify and analyze the target customer base — including consumer buyer personas, organizational
markets, and demographics — and demonstrate how content strategy is informed by and aligned to
audience needs
5. Develop and execute effective content creation strategies across relevant social media platforms,
ensuring consistency, engagement, and alignment with campaign goals
6. Utilize fixed financial resources strategically within a marketing environment to maximize campaign
reach and impact
7. Demonstrate an understanding of how digital marketing influences and responds to shifts in
consumer demographics and behavior
8. Demonstrate the teamwork skills required to collaborate effectively and perform at a high level in a
professional, world -class workforce environment

Contest Specifications for this Event

• The social media marketing campaign must not exceed ten (10) pages single -sided (excluding the Title
Page, Table of Contents, and Works Cited) and must follow the Social Media Marketing Campaign
format in the Style & Reference Manual . Any marketing plan submitted beyond the maximum number
of pages will be disqualified.
• Supporting documentation (research, charts, brochures, etc.) is not included in the marketing plan but
should be shared during the presentation.
• Submit the Social Media Marketing Campaign, the Works Cited, BPA Release Form(s), and AI Usage
and Documentation Form in a combined PDF file to https://upload.bpa.org no later than
11:59 p.m.
Eastern Time on January 15, 2027 . No changes can be made to the project after the date of submission.

• Advisors of qualifying teams will be contacted to reserve a time for a videoconference presentation
before a panel of judges. The dates and times for presentation reservations will be announced via
email notification. Teams will be assigned to sections pri or to the preliminary presentation.
• Length of event: no more than three (3) minutes for setup, no more than ten (10) minutes for the
presentation, and no more than five (5) minutes for judges’ questions.
• The top teams with the highest cumulative technical and presentation scores will be invited to attend
the National Leadership Conference to be recognized for their outstanding efforts and to participate
in the National Showcase.

Common Specifications for all BPA Judged Events

• Team ID is required for all submissions. Materials from non -registered members and/or those
missing team ID cannot be accepted.
• The team is responsible for securing a BPA Release Form(s) from any person, including
themselves, whose image or work is used in the project.
• The team is responsible for citing all sources, including themselves, on the Works Cited page for
any work used on the project. It is the policy of Business Professionals of America to comply with
copyright law. The Style & Reference Manual contains guidelines for Copyright and/or Fair Use.
Contestants will be disqualified for violations of the guidelines.
• Materials must follow the organization’s BPA Graphic Standards and make proper use of the BPA logo
and/or organization’s name. Refer to the BPA Graphic Standards in the Style & Reference Manual.
• Judges’ comments will be returned digitally through the online judging system at the national level.
Materials submitted for technical judging will not be returned and will not be available at NLC.
