Description & Eligibility
Contestants will demonstrate practical knowledge of configuring, maintaining, and troubleshooting devices
as an IT technician. This event focuses on real -world technical skills used in supporting and managing
modern hardware and operating environments. Any secondary or postsecondary division contestant may
enter this objective event.

Contestant Must Supply
• Published and/or unpublished non -electronic written reference materials, including hands -on tasks
• (Optional) Cordless calculator: Electronic devices will be monitored according to ACT standards.
See NLC Calculator Guidelines .  Contestants who violate this rule will be disqualified.

• Computer and software

Contest Competencies
1.
Install, upgrade, and safely replace hardware components to maintain, optimize, and extend device
performance and lifecycle
2. Identify and connect peripheral devices using the appropriate ports and interfaces, install and
update device drivers, and diagnose and resolve connectivity, compatibility, and performance
issues
3. Configure and manage storage devices and technologies, allocate and optimize system resources,
and troubleshoot storage-related and resource -related performance issues
4. Diagnose hardware and software issues, troubleshoot boot procedures, and perform preventative
maintenance to minimize downtime and ensure system reliability
