Description & Eligibility
Contestants will demonstrate a basic understanding of spelling, grammar and writing skills for business
communication. Any middle level contestant may enter this objective event.

Contestant Must Supply
• Optional: Sharpened pencils, pens
• Cordless calculator: Electronic devices will be monitored according to ACT standards. See NLC
Calculator Guidelines. Contestants who violate this rule will be disqualified.

Resources
• Webster’s New Collegiate Dictionary

Contest Notes
• Method of evaluation: objective
• AI Policy Level 1 No AI Allowed
• Reference materials are not allowed
• Length of event: no more than sixty (60) minutes testing time
• Unlimited entries by state

Contest Competencies
1.
Analyze professional written communications for clarity and accuracy, applying correct grammar,
spelling, and tone across digital and traditional formats
2. Effectively analyze the use of verbal, nonverbal, and electronic communication
3. Demonstrate the importance of maintaining positive relationships in work environments
4. Demonstrate the job application process using digital platforms, networking tools, and application
strategies
