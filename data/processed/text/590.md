## Page 1

590 Marketing & Sales Concepts (Open)
(formerly, in part, 591 Management, Marketing and HR Concepts)

Description & Eligibility
Contestants will demonstrate foundational knowledge of
Marketing & Sales concepts including market
research, target market analysis, pricing strategies, promotional planning, consumer behavior, and sales
processes. Any secondary or postsecondary division contestant may enter this objective event.

Contestant Must Supply
• (Optional) Cordless calculator: Electronic devices will be monitored according to ACT standards.
See NLC Calculator Guidelines .  Contestants who violate this rule will be disqualified.

Contest Notes
• Method of evaluation: objective
• AI Policy Level 1 No AI Allowed
• Reference materials are not allowed
• Length of event: no more than sixty (60) minutes testing time
• Unlimited entries by state

Contest Competencies

1.
Demonstrate understanding of core marketing principles, including the marketing mix, branding,
and the role of marketing in the global business environment
2. Analyze target markets and consumer behavior using demographic, psychographic, and buying
behavior data, and interpret market research findings to support informed
Marketing & Sales
decisions
3. Develop and evaluate pricing strategies based on cost, competition, and customer value

4. Apply knowledge of integrated marketing communications across digital and traditional channels,
including social media platforms, influencer marketing, search engine optimization (SEO), email
marketing, content marketing, pay -per-click advertising, and pro motional strategies to build brand
awareness and engage target audiences
5. Understand sales processes and techniques, including prospecting, presenting, handling
objections, closing, and building long -term customer relationships
6. Apply ethical, legal, and socially responsible practices in all
Marketing & Sales activities
