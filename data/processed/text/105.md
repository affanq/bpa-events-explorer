105 College Accounting

Description & Eligibility
Contestants will apply accounting principles to record transactions,
prepare and analyze financial
statements, and evaluate business structures to support accurate reporting and informed decision -
making. Any postsecondary division contestant may enter this application event. Contestants may not
enter College Accounting and Advanced College Accounting in the same year.
This event may not be
repeated.

• Published and/or unpublished non -electronic written reference materials

NLC
Calculator Guidelines . Contestants who violate this rule will be disqualified

Contest Competencies

1.
Analyze and compare business ownership structures to evaluate strategic, legal, and operational
implications
2. Apply the accounting cycle to record and report financial data in compliance with industry
standards and decision-making needs
3. Apply financial standards to assess and report the value of assets, liabilities, and equity in
accordance with GAAP
4. Interpret foundational accounting concepts and terminology to explain the role of accounting
systems in supporting financial accuracy, compliance, and business decision-making
5. Evaluate, journalize, and post financial transactions to determine their impact on assets, liabilities,
equity, revenues, expenses, and dividends in accordance with GAAP
6. Apply multiple depreciation methods to calculate asset value in compliance with financial
reporting standards
7. Calculate and record end-of-period adjustments to ensure accurate financial reporting and
compliance with accrual accounting principles
8. Maintain inventory records using perpetual accounting systems and apply inventory costing
methods to ensure accurate valuation and cost flow analysis
9. Evaluate and manage credit risk, including estimating and recording uncollectible accounts using
data-driven methods
10. Calculate and analyze interest and maturity values for various financial instruments
11. Prepare, interpret, and analyze financial statements for service, merchandising, and digital business models
12. Analyze financial performance using key ratios and dashboards, integrating data
visualization tools and benchmarking techniques
