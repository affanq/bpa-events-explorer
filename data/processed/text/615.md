Description & Eligibility
Contestants will demonstrate communication skills in securing, arranging, organizing, and presenting a
multimedia presentation on the provided topic. Any secondary or postsecondary division contestant may
enter this judged event. Entries that do not follow the provided topic or the Copyright and/or Fair Use
Guidelines and Artificial Intelligence Usage & Citation Policy will be disqualified.

Topic
Accelerating Pediatric Cancer Research Through Collaboration
. You are part of a healthcare foundation
focused on advancing childhood cancer research. Prepare a presentation examining how collaboration
among hospitals, researchers, pharmaceutical companies, nonprofit organizations, and patient advocacy
groups contributes to medical breakthroughs. Analyze challenges that limit collaboration and data sharing.

Evaluate the impact of research partnerships on treatment development and patient outcomes. Finally,
recommend strategies to strengthen collaboration and accelerate the development of new therapies for
children with cancer.

Contestant Must Supply
• One (1) copy of the signed BPA Release Form(s), Works Cited, Fair Use Guidelines and AI Usage
and Documentation Form for each round of the competition (preliminaries and finals). No
materials, other than the required submission materials, may be left with judges.
• Students may use one single-sided laptop, notebook, or tablet for their presentation. Projection
equipment and external displays will not be provided or permitted.

Competition Notes
• Method of evaluation: judged
• AI Policy Level 2 Permitted with Citation
• Carry -in and setup of equipment must be done solely by the contestant and must take place within
the time allotted
• No Internet access will be provided on -site at NLC; however, contestants may provide their own
access to be used only for their presentation to the judges
• Length of event: no more than three (3) minutes for setup, no less than seven (7) and no more than
ten (10) minutes for oral presentation, no more than five (5) minutes for judges’ questions
• Finals may be part of the competition
• Each state is allowed three (3) entries

Contest Competencies
1.
Conduct comprehensive research using diverse resources and communicate findings through
professional reports and presentations, responding to questions with evidence -based reasoning
2. Design and deliver engaging multimedia presentations using advanced digital tools and cloud -
based platforms
3. Demonstrate effective communication skills while presenting a multimedia presentation

Contest Specifications for this Event

• Presentation should make effective use of current multimedia technology (e.g., sound, movement,
digital video, etc.)
• Contestant should use space, color, and text as design factors
• The use of graphics, including charts, should be included in the presentation
• Contestant ID is required for all submissions. Materials from non -registered contestants and/or
those missing Contestant ID cannot be accepted
