Description & Eligibility
Contestants will demonstrate understanding of the core principles and practices of professional meeting
and event management. This objective test reflects industry -informed standards and emphasizes ethical
conduct, effective communication, and appropriate use of technology. Any secondary or postsecondary
division contestant may enter this objective event.

Contestant Must Supply
• (Optional) Cordless calculator: Electronic devices will be monitored according to ACT standards.
See NLC Calculator Guidelines.  Contestants who violate this rule will be disqualified
.

Contest Notes
• Method of evaluation: objective
• AI Policy Level 1 No AI Allowed
• Reference materials are not allowed. Contestants who violate this rule will be disqualified.
• Length of event: no more than sixty (60) minutes testing time
• Unlimited entries by state

Contest Competencies

1.
Plan and manage all aspects of an event project by organizing tasks, timelines, and resources

2. Recruit, schedule, and train staff or volunteers to support event operations

3. Manage the event budget by tracking expenses, handling monetary transactions, and making cost
-
effective decisions
4. Demonstrate knowledge of event design principles by planning the theme, program, agenda, room
layout, speakers, performers, and key activities
5. Coordinate food, beverage, and vendor services according to event needs and expectations

6. Plan attendee flow and manage the event site by selecting an appropriate venue, designing the
layout, and ensuring efficient movement and operations
7. Develop and manage event communication materials, including marketing pieces, signage, and
merchandise
8. Communicate clearly and professionally in written and verbal business interactions

9. Demonstrate professionalism by acting responsibly, ethically, respectfully, and with transparency

10. Identify potential risks and develop basic contingency plans for unexpected challenges

11. Apply sustainable and socially responsible practices in meeting and event planning when
appropriate
12. Evaluate event success using attendee feedback, budget outcomes, and key performance
indicators
