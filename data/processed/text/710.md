710 Advanced Word Processing
(formerly 210)

Description & Eligibility
Contestants will demonstrate advanced -level word processing skills by creating, editing, and formatting a
variety of business documents with accuracy. Any secondary or postsecondary division contestant may
enter this application event. Contestants may comp ete in only one of the following events each year:
Fundamental Word Processing, Intermediate Word Processing, or Advanced Word Processing. This event
may be repeated.

Contestant Must Supply
• Published and/or unpublished non -electronic written reference materials

Equipment/Supplies Provided

• Computer and software

Contest Notes
• Method of evaluation: application
• AI Policy Level 1 No AI Allowed
• All contestant-produced jobs completed on -site are uploaded electronically and are not printed at
the national level
• Previous Business Professionals of America tests and/or sample tests (handwritten, photocopied or
keyed) may not be taken into the testing area. Violation of this rule will result in disqualification.

• Length of event: no more than ten (10) minutes orientation, no more than ninety (90) minutes testing
time, and no more than ten (10) minutes wrap-up
o No more than fifty (50) minutes for certification test
o At NLC, contestants will take MOS Word 365 Expert certification as part of the event.
Contestants passing this certification will have 50 points added to their event score. A
Pearson profile must be created at https://certiport.pearsonvue.com before attending
NLC
o Contestants must include their BPA Contestant ID in their Pearson profile when they
register
o Certiport Parent/Legal Guardian Consent Form must be uploaded to:
education@bpa.org
o For more information on the exam, visit https://certiport.pearsonvue.com
o Reference materials are not allowed for certification testing
• Entries vary by state

Contest Competencies

1. Create, format, and manage diverse business documents using word processing tools and features such
as styles, templates, headers/footers, section breaks, mail merge and multi
-column layouts
2. Demonstrate comprehensive knowledge of word processing software functions, including automation
features, utilizing digital reference and collaborative editing tools

3. Integrate and format data using tables, charts, and infographics
4. Insert visual elements applying accessibility standards and advanced formatting tools
