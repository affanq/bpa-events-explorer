Description & Eligibility
Contestants will demonstrate job search and interview readiness through professional communication,
employability skills, and career preparation. Contestants will create a resume and cover letter, research
career qualifications, and participate in a formal interview. Evaluation will focus on written and oral
communication, professional image, workplace ethics, and interpersonal effectiveness in an in
-person
setting. Any secondary or postsecondary division contestant may enter this application event.
Contestants
may not enter both Interview Skills and Advanced Interview Skills in the same year. This event may
not be
repeated. Contestants must be registered for the national competition before the submission deadline
and compete in both parts of the event to be ranked.

Contestant Must Supply
• One (1) copy of your resume and cover letter submitted at presentation check
-in (both
preliminaries and finals)

Contest Notes
• Method of evaluation: judged
• AI Policy Level 1 No AI Allowed
• Length of event: no more than fifteen (15) minutes for interview
• Each state is allowed three (3) entries
• Finals may be part of the competition
