Description & Eligibility
Contestants will assess understanding and application of human resource management principles through
analysis and interpretation of workplace scenarios. Contestants will demonstrate knowledge of employment
law, ethical leadership, talent management, and s trategic HR functions while applying effective communication
and decision-making skills. Evaluation will emphasize professionalism, HR strategy, legal compliance, and
support for organizational culture and employee well -being. Any secondary or postsecondar y division
contestant may enter this judged event. The event may be repeated.

Contestant Must Supply
• Human Resources Manual
• Sharpened pencils, pens

Equipment/Supplies Provided

• Three (3) notecards per contestant
• Scenario

Contest Notes
• Method of evaluation: judged
• AI Policy Level 1 No AI Allowed
• Length of event: no more than twenty (20) minutes preparation time, no less than three (3) and no
more than five (5) minutes for oral presentation, no more than three (3) minutes judges’ questions
• Finals may be part of the competition
• Each state is allowed three (3) entries

Contest Competencies

1.
Communicate HR concepts and recommendations clearly and professionally using data, policy
references, and supportive reasoning
2. Organize the presentation using a clear introduction, body, and conclusion to create a coherent and
persuasive HR message
3. Use effective verbal and nonverbal delivery skills, including vocal variety, posture, gestures, and eye
contact—to support confident and professional HR communication

4. Model professional behaviors that support the organization’s mission and values
5. Describe and apply workplace safety, health, and compliance requirements

6. Model ethical, legal, and responsible conduct by maintaining confidentiality, understanding liability,
and supporting organizational integrity
7. Demonstrate technical HR knowledge across areas such as staffing, training, compliance, and
employee relations
8. Analyze HR issues and apply relevant policies to develop fair, compliant, and effective solutions

9. Demonstrate respectful, fair, and professional interactions in workplace scenarios

10. Follow laws and professional guidelines to ensure your actions protect both yourself and the
organization in HR settings
11. Respond to judge questions clearly and confidently, using BPA’s Human Resources Manual, scenario
details, examples, and sound HR reasoning from your preparation

Contest Specifications for this Event

• The contestant will be given a human resources management scenario. The scenario may deal with
office situations, workplace issues, legal matters, etc.
• The event proctor will provide three (3) notecards. Only the
