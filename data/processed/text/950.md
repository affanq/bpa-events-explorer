950 Video Production Team

Description & Eligibility
Teams will create a one to two (1:00-2:00) minute video based on the assigned topic. Any middle level
contestant may enter this judged team event. A team will consist of 2 -4 contestants. Teams must be registered
for the national competition before the submission deadline and compete in both parts of the event to be
ranked. Entries that do not follow the provided topic
, the Copyright and/or Fair Use Guidelines or Artificial
Intelligence Usage & Citation Policy will be disqualified.

Topic
Create a one -to-two-minute educational video that explores how a specific piece of technology has changed
over time — from its early beginnings to today. Your goal is to entertain and inform your audience while telling
the story of innovation in a creative , visually engaging way.

Your Video Must Include:
1.
Voice-Over Narration
• Write and record a voice -over that walks viewers through the history and evolution of your chosen
technology
• Use expressive tone and pacing to make history feel exciting and relevant
• You may narrate as yourself, as a time traveler, a historian, or another creative character
2. At Least One "Live" Recorded Scene

• Film at least one scene featuring real people interacting with old or new versions of
technology
• This could be a skit comparing past vs. present, a demonstration, or a fun reenactment of how
people used technology in another era
3. Fun Fact Segment
• Include at least one surprising or interesting fact about your chosen technology

• This can be delivered as a text overlay, graphic, or spoken moment in the narration

• Make sure it connects back to your theme and adds value to the viewer's understanding

Team Must Supply
• Students may use one single -sided laptop, notebook, or tablet for their presentation. Projection
equipment and external displays will not be provided or permitted.

Contest Notes
• Method of evaluation: judged
• AI Policy Level 1 No AI Allowed
• Carry-in and setup of equipment must be done solely by the team and must take place within the time
allotted
• No Internet access will be provided on -site at NLC; however, teams may provide their own access to
be used only for their presentation to the judges
• Length of event : no more than three (3) minutes for setup , no more than five (5) minutes for the
presentation, n o more than five (5) minutes for judges’ questions
• Finals may be part of the competition
• Each state is allowed three (3) entries
