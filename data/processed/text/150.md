Topic
Lifted Technologies is a startup developing AI -powered diagnostic tools for rural healthcare providers. The
company offers a subscription -based software platform (SaaS) and specialized medical scanning
equipment to clinics underserved by traditional healthcare infrastructure.

After three years of operations,  Lifted  Technologies has gained a strong client base in the Midwest and
Southeast and is now seeking Series B funding to scale nationally. The company carries significant
investment in patents and equipment as it continues to invest heavily in research and development.
Leadership must now decide between pursuing venture capital funding, securing a bank loan, or entering a
strategic partnership with a larger healthcare company to finance their national expansion.

Contest Competencies

1.
Apply accounting principles to evaluate and report the value of assets, liabilities, and owner’s
equity in accordance with GAAP
2. Prepare, interpret, and analyze financial statements to assess performance and support strategic
decision-making across various business models
3. Apply planning and control principles, including budgeting, forecasting, differential analysis, and
present-value concepts, to evaluate organizational performance and
guide financial decisions for
both the short and long -term
4. Evaluate credit options, costs, and legal implications, and identify financial risks to inform business
decisions and develop mitigation strategies
5. Communicate financial insights clearly, courteously, and professionally to diverse stakeholders

6. Use ratio analysis to evaluate comparative financial statements and assess organizational trends
and performance
7. Apply statistical procedures and utilize spreadsheet software to analyze, interpret, and model
financial data efficiently and accurately
8. Present financial findings using formal presentations and supporting materials to communicate
insights and recommendations effectively
