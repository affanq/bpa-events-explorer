V13 Virtual Esports Team

Description and Eligibility
Teams will research and develop strategies to effectively compete in a virtual esports competition. Competing
teams will submit a research paper on the provided esports topic, participate in an esports tournament, and
deliver a presentation highlighting the strategies employed and lessons learned. This national event will be
submitted and judged virtually. Any middle level, secondary, or postsecondary contestant
may enter this
event. A team will consist of 2 –4 contestants, depending on the division. There are no restrictions on the
number of entries per chapter or per state. Teams that do not submit an entry following the topic listed below
or do not follow the Co pyright and/or Fair Use Guidelines and the Artificial Intelligence Usage and Citation
Policy will be disqualified.

Topic
The Business of Producing Esports Events.
Esports tournaments range from local competitions to international
championships with millions of viewers. In a research paper of no more than three pages (double
-spaced),
examine the business operations involved in planning, marketing, and managing esport s events.

Research Areas:
• Event planning and logistics
• Sponsorship acquisition and revenue generation
• Broadcasting and live -stream production
• Risk management and cybersecurity considerations
• Career opportunities in event operations and production

Contest Competencies

1.
Analyze esports scenarios, gameplay data, and game characteristics to troubleshoot strategies,
develop visualizations, and refine and execute strategic plans for effective competitive gameplay

2. Define and analyze how individual player personalities, play styles, and roles
—including patterns
observed in professional players —contribute to team dynamics and improve overall competitive
performance
3. Create and communicate short - and long-term individual and team goals, incorporating time -
management strategies that balance esports, academics, and personal responsibilities

4. Demonstrate effective technical reading, writing, and verbal/non -verbal communication skills to
support collaboration, research, and professional presentation
5. Demonstrate healthy gaming concepts and strategies in local and virtual environments, promoting
physical, mental, and social well -being in competitive settings
6. Demonstrate knowledge of intellectual property laws, legal responsibilities, and ethical standards in
esports and gaming—including copyright, trademarks, DMCA, and patents
—and evaluate how esports
technology and social media have influenced culture, consum er behavior, and industry growth
