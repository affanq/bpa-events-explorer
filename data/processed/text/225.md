Description & Eligibility
Teams will learn to use industry -standard software for word processing, spreadsheets, databases,
presentations, and publishing to create professional materials and streamline workflows. Teams will
develop teamwork and leadership strategies while ensuring a ll communications meet organizational
branding standards. A team will consist of 2 -4 contestants. Any secondary or postsecondary division
contestant may enter this application event.

Team Must Supply
• Published and/or unpublished non -electronic written reference materials

Equipment/Supplies Provided

• Computer with software (one per contestant)
• Flash Drive (one per team, to be submitted with test materials after testing)

Contest Notes
• Method of evaluation: application
• AI Policy Level 1 No AI Allowed
• All student-produced jobs completed on -site are uploaded electronically and are not printed at
the national level
• Previous Business Professionals of America tests and/or sample tests (handwritten, photocopied or
keyed) may not be taken into the testing area. Violation of this rule will result in disqualification.

• Length of event: no more than ten (10) minutes orientation, no more than ninety (90) minutes testing
time, and no more than ten (10) minutes wrap-up
• Each state is allowed three (3) entries

Contest Competencies

1.
Utilize industry-standard software for word processing, database management, spreadsheets,
presentations, and desktop publishing to organize and merge data, analyze information, and create
professional business materials across multiple applications to ens
ure efficiency
2. Demonstrate leadership and teamwork by evaluating tasks, delegating responsibilities, and
managing priorities and timelines to optimize workflow and achieve organizational goals
3. Ensure all communications and materials adhere to organizational style and branding standards
