430 Video Production Team

Description & Eligibility
Contestants will create a three to five (3:00 -5:00) minute video production, based on the provided topic. Any
secondary or postsecondary division contestant may enter this judged team event. A team will consist of 2 -4
contestants. Teams must be registered for the national competition before the submission deadline and
compete in both parts of the event to be ranked. Entries that do not follow the provided topic or the
Copyright and/or Fair Use Guidelines and Artificial Intelligence Usage & Citation Policy will be disqualified.

Topic
Create a three to five (3:00 -5:00) minute video that reveals what is not usually seen behind digital spaces such
as social media, gaming, artificial intelligence, influencers, or online learning. Include a script and storyboard.
Your video should help viewers understand the hidden work, challenges, or realities that exist behind digital
platforms and online experiences.
The video must include interactive elements that allow the viewer to explore different layers of information or
perspectives. Suggested interactive elements (but not limited to): QR Codes, Calls -to-action (Visit
our website or follow us on social media), Polls or questions displayed in the video.
Videos should demonstrate creativity, effective storytelling, and appropriate use of interactive digital media
elements. Topics must be appropriate for a high school audience and align with the mission and values of
Business Professionals of America.
Team Must Supply
• Students may use one single-sided laptop, notebook, or tablet for their presentation. Projection
equipment and external displays will not be provided or permitted .
• (National only) Film equipment (computer, software, camera, etc.)
• Optional – Props

Competition Notes
• Method of evaluation: judged
• AI Policy Level 1 No AI Allowed
• Carry -in and setup of equipment must be done solely by the contestant within the time allotted
• No Internet access will be provided on -site at NLC; however, contestants may provide their own
access to be used only for their presentation to the judges
• No materials may be left with the judges
• Only the team captain should complete the submission
• Length of event: no more than three (3) minutes for setup, no more than ten (10) minutes for the
presentation, and no more than five (5) minutes for judges’ questions
• Finals may be part of the competition
• Each state is allowed three (3) entries

Contest Competencies
1.
Apply audience analysis and user -centered design principles & elements to create and refine
storylines using digital storyboarding and scripting, ensuring clear messaging and engagement for a
defined target audience across web, social, and mobile platforms
2. Utilize video production software, hardware, and workflows to plan, capture, and edit high-quality
video content, incorporating nonlinear editing techniques, transitions, audio mixing, sound design,
titles, and visual effects to enhance storytelling and pr oduction value
3. Employ camera and lighting techniques—including shot composition, movement, framing, and modern
lighting setups—to capture visually engaging footage that meets professional standards

Contest Specifications for this Event

• Teams may utilize audio, text, video, graphics and animation. Use of transitions and continuity must
exist in the production
• Presentation should focus on the concept, development and production of the video
• No presentation for the finals round at the National Leadership Conference. Scoring from the
preliminary round will not be included in finals scoring. Video is judged on technical merit only.
o Teams will have no more than four (4) hours to plan the storyline and complete all production
phases including filming and exporting the video.
o During this time, students may communicate with contestants of their team via telephone but
may not communicate with others (including their advisor) concerning the production of their
video. Teams will be disqualified for violating this rule.
o After filming is complete, teams will return to the contest room to edit and complete all
production and post -production elements in their video.
o The video must be one (1) minute in length, rendered and exported into a MOV, MP4, AVI,
MKV or MPG format and submitted using the provided flash drive. If video is not in the
appropriate format, it may not be judged.
