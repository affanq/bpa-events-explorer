Description & Eligibility
Contestants will demonstrate essential spreadsheet skills to organize, analyze, and present data with
precision. Learn to design and format workbooks, build automated solutions using formulas and
references, create charts, and apply styles and validation f
or professional, user-friendly reports. Any
secondary or postsecondary division contestant may enter this application event. Contestants may
not
enter Fundamental and Advanced Spreadsheet Applications in the same year. This event may
not be
repeated.

Contest Competencies

1.
Design, format, and manage worksheets and workbooks using spreadsheet tools to organize,
analyze, and present data accurately and efficiently
2. Develop formulas/functions to create automated spreadsheet solutions

3. Apply mixed, absolute, and relative cell references, including cross
-sheet references
4. Create charts and organize data using outlines, groups, and subtotals to enhance reporting

5. Enhance spreadsheet usability and presentation by applying styles, validating data, auditing
formulas, and customizing print options
