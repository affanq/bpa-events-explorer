Topic
Global supply chain disruptions and geopolitical tensions  are causing some political and business leaders
to push the U.S. toward reshoring manufacturing and economic deglobalization.   Is this  a sustainable
strategy for long-term growth in the U.S.?

Considerations
• The trade-offs between domestic production and global economic interdependence.

• Costs and benefits of trade protectionism and tariff policies
vs the efficiency of global trade.
• Circular flow of the economy and how reshoring affects production and income.

• Labor market impacts of returning manufacturing jobs to the United States

• Effects on consumer prices when domestic production replaces cheaper imports
