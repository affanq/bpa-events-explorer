Topic: Launch of an Esports Team

A new fictional esports team is preparing to compete in national gaming tournaments and needs a strong
promotional theme to build fan excitement and team recognition. Your task is to develop and illustrate a
cohesive theme that reflects the team’s identity , competitive spirit, and gaming culture.

The theme should appeal to a primary audience of teen gamers ages 13–18. All designs must be original and
may not replicate or closely imitate existing esports team logos, trademarks, or copyrighted materials.

Task:
Develop and illustrate the theme by creating the following promotional materials:
• One Page Branding Document (including colors, fonts, and logo usage)
• Team Logo Design
• Esports Team Jersey Design (front and back)
• Promotional Poster sized 24” by 36” inches (announcing the launch of the esports team and its
entry into competition)
The theme should be applied consistently across all promotional materials, creating a cohesive look
through color, typography, layout, and graphics.

Contestant Must Supply
• Optional – Design printouts
• Students may use one single-sided laptop, notebook, or tablet for their presentation. Projection
equipment and external displays will not be provided or permitted .

presentation, and no more than five (5) minutes for judges’ questions
• Finals may be part of the competition
• Each state is allowed three (3) entries
