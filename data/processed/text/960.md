960 Visual Design Team

Description & Eligibility
Teams will use UX and UI designs to develop mockups that are visually and functionally aligned with the
assigned topic . A team will consist of 2 -4 contestants. Teams must be registered for the national competition
before the submission deadline and compete in both parts of the event to be ranked.
Entries that do not
follow the provided topic or the Copyright and/or Fair Use Guidelines or Artificial Intelligence Usage &
Citation Policy will be disqualified.

Topic
Your team has been hired to design a complete branding package for your school's first annual
Fresh Picks
Farmers Market — a student-organized event celebrating local food, sustainability, and community. The
branding should feel fresh, colorful, and welcom ing for all ages. Your package must include a minimum of four
(4) items, and the following item is required:
1.
Event Poster (Required) . A full-sized promotional poster that includes the event name, date, time, and
location, along with engaging visuals that capture the spirit of a community farmers market.

Additional items may include any three (3) the following:
2. Event Logo . A unique logo representing the Fresh Picks Farmers Market brand, suitable for use across
all marketing materials.
3. Vendor Table Sign . A designed sign for a student vendor booth that includes the
vendor's name and a
short description of what they are selling.
4. Reusable Shopping Bag Graphic . A design for a branded reusable tote bag that could be sold or given
away at the event.
5. Social Media Announcement Graphic . A graphic promoting the event on social media, including the
event details and a call to action.
6. Event Program / Map . A designed program or layout map showing vendor locations and event
activities.

Ensure all designs are visually cohesive, vibrant, and communicate a clear and inviting message about the
event.

Team Must Supply
• Students may use one single -sided laptop, notebook, or tablet for their presentation. Projection
equipment and external displays will not be provided or permitted.

Contest Notes
• Method of evaluation: judged
• AI Policy Level 2 Permitted with Citation
• Carry-in and setup of equipment must be done solely by the team and must take place within the time
allotted
• No Internet access will be provided on -site at NLC; however, teams may provide their own access to
be used only for their presentation to the judges
• Length of event: no more than three (3) minutes for setup, no more than ten (10) minutes for the
presentation, no more than five (5) minutes for judges’ questions
• Finals may be part of the competition
• Each state is allowed three (3) entries
