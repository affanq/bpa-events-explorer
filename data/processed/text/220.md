Description & Eligibility
Contestants will develop essential skills for a professional legal environment. Learn to apply legal
terminology, create and manage accurate documents, maintain compliance with data protection and
ethical standards, and organize office systems using indust ry protocols. Gain experience in client service,
legal research, and understanding court procedures. Any secondary or postsecondary division contestant
may enter this application event.

Contestant Must Supply
• Published and/or unpublished non -electronic written reference materials
• Suggested resource: ANSI/ARMA 1 -1995 - Alphabetic Filing Rules

Equipment/Supplies Provided

• Computer and software

Contest Notes
• Method of evaluation: application
• AI Policy Level 1 No AI Allowed
• All student-produced jobs completed on -site are uploaded electronically and are not printed at
the national level
• Previous Business Professionals of America tests and/or sample tests (handwritten, photocopied or
keyed) may not be taken into the testing area. Violation of this rule will result in disqualification.

• Length of event: no more than ten (10) minutes orientation, no more than sixty (60) minutes testing
time, and no more than ten (10) minutes wrap-up
• Entries vary by state

Contest Competencies

1.
Apply foundational legal terminology, including Latin terms, and office procedures to ensure
accurate communication and compliance in a legal environment
2. Create, edit, and format legal documents and client correspondence using digital tools and
industry-standard practices to ensure accuracy, clarity, and compliance

3. Manage electronic communications and files in compliance with legal protocols and data
protection regulations
4. Deliver professional client service while upholding ethical standards and confidentiality
requirements in all legal office interactions
5. Organize and maintain office inventory and filing systems using digital tools and ARMA standards

6. Research legal information and manage client records and billing procedures

7. Apply knowledge of federal, state, and local court structures and proceedings
