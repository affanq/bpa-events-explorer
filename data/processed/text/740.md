Description & Eligibility
Contestants will d emonstrate effective communication skills by arranging, organizing, and presenting
information orally without prior knowledge of the topic. The speech may serve one or more purposes: to
impress, to inform, or to persuade. Any postsecondary division contestant may enter this
judged event. The
event may be repeated.

Contestant Must Supply
• Sharpened pencils, pens

Equipment/Supplies Provided

• Three (3) notecards per contestant
• Topics

Contest Notes
• Method of evaluation: judged
• AI Policy Level 1 No AI Allowed
• Length of event: no more than ten (10) minutes preparation, no less than three (3) and no more than
five (5) minutes speech
• Finals may be part of the competition
• Each state is allowed three (3) entries

Contest Competencies

1.
Demonstrate clear, professional, and effective oral communication when presenting ideas

2. Organize the speech using a clear introduction, body, and conclusion to create a coherent message

3. Use appropriate tempo, pitch, and vocal variety to enhance clarity and audience engagement

4. Use purposeful nonverbal gestures, posture, and eye contact to support confident delivery

5. Analyze the assigned topic quickly and develop key points supported by logical reasoning, examples,
and relevant business insights
6. Adapt tone, language, and delivery to meet audience expectations
7. Use effective opening and closing statements to strengthen the overall message

8. Demonstrate professionalism, confidence, and ethical communication while presenting under pressure

Contest Specifications for this Event

• The contestant will draw two (2) different business topics and will select either one. The topics may
deal with Business Professionals of America, office situations, the business world, etc.

• The event proctor will provide three (3) notecards. Notes can only be made on the notecards
provided.
• No advisor contact will be allowed between the time of receiving the topic and the delivery

• No materials or previously prepared notes will be allowed into the preparation or presentation room
with the contestant
• No personal electronic devices (smart watches, smart glasses, cell phone, etc.)

• The contestant will be given warnings via flash cards when there are two (2) minutes remaining and
when there is (1) minute remaining during the speaking time
