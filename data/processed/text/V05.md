V05 Virtual Mobile Applications

Description & Eligibility
Contestants will develop a mobile phone and/or tablet application based upon the given scenario. Permitted
platforms are limited to Google Android ™ or Apple iOS ™. This national event will be submitted and judged
virtually. Any middle level, secondary or postsecondary contestant may enter this event.  There are no
restrictions on the number of entries per chapter or per state. Contestants who do not submit an entry
following the topic listed below or do not follow the Copyright and/or Fair Use Guidelines and the Artificial
Intelligence Usage and Citation policy will be disqualified.

Topic
SafelyGo – Student Campus Safety App Develop a mobile application titled SafelyGo. Student Campus Safety
App. You are challenged to create a mobile app that promotes student safety on and around campus. The app
should allow users to report safety concerns, access emergency contacts, share real
-time location with trusted
contacts, and view a live campus safety map.

Required Features: external backend database, user registration and account recovery, ability to
submit and
view safety reports, emergency contact directory, location sharing, and an
admin panel for campus safety staff
to manage reports. Must use at least one API (e.g., maps or SMS/notification service).
