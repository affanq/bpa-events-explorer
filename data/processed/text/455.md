455 User Experience Design Team

Description & Eligibility
The team will prototype digital experiences for a single brand based on the assigned topic, emphasizing
collaboration, user experience design, and design rationale. Any secondary or postsecondary division
contestant may enter this judged team event. A team will consist of 2 -4 contestants. Teams must be
registered for the national competition before the submission deadline and compete in both parts of the
event to be ranked. Entries that do not follow the provided topic or the Copyright and/or Fair Use
Guidelines and the Artificial Intelligence Usage and Citation Policy will be disqualified.

Topic
Social Media Detox & Screen Balance App . BalanceMe is a digital wellness experience designed for Gen Z,
built around awareness, intention, and emotional well-being. Instead of restricting screen time or shaming
users, BalanceMe helps students build healthier digital habits through personalized insights, m ood checkins, flexible goals, and encouraging nudges. Our educational website teaches practical digital wellness
strategies, while our app and social media campaign promote balanced, intentional technology use in a
positive, relatable way.

Your team has been hired as a digital experience design agency to design a prototype that educates users,
supports self-awareness, and promotes balance across devices and platforms.

Deliverables:
• Educational website explaining digital wellness concepts, healthy technology habits, and the
purpose of the app.
• Mobile app prototype with five required screens:
o Screen time overview
o Goal setting
o Reminders and nudges
o Focus mode
o Reflection and insights
• Social media awareness campaign artboard designed for Instagram or TikTok that promotes
healthy screen balance messaging and drives users to the app or website.

Team Must Supply

equipment and external displays will not be provided or permitted .
