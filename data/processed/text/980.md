980 Prepared Speech

Description & Eligibility
Contestants will demonstrate professional public speaking and research
-based communication skills by
preparing and delivering a well -organized speech on a business, leadership, or professional development
topic. Contestants will research credible sources, demonstrate understanding of their findings, and present a
logically structured speech using effective verbal and nonverbal communication techniques. Evaluation will
emphasize organization, delivery, audience engagement, ethical source use, and professionalism. The event
may be repeated, but the topic may not.  Contestants may not enter Extemporaneous Speech, Presentation
Team, or Prepared Speech in the same year.

Contestant Must Supply
• One (1) copy of the Speech Outline, Works Cited formatted according to the
Style & Reference
Manual and BPA Release Form(s) , and AI Usage and Documentation Form for each round (prelims and
finals)
• Optional - Easel, flipchart, posterboard and props

Contest Notes
• Method of evaluation: judged
• AI Policy Level 2 Permitted with Citation
• Length of event : no more than three (3) minutes setup , no less than three (3) and no more five (5)
minute presentation
• Finals may be included at state and national levels
• Each state is allowed three (3) entries

Contest Competencies
1.
Communicate ideas clearly, professionally, and confidently through effective speaking skills

2. Organize the speech using a clear introduction, body, and conclusion to create a coherent and
engaging message
3. Use appropriate tempo, pitch, vocal variety, gestures, posture, and eye contact to support confident
and professional delivery
4. Incorporate research, evidence, and credible sources to strengthen key points and support the overall
message
5. Adapt content, tone, and delivery to meet the needs and expectations of the intended audience

6. Demonstrate professionalism and ethical communication by using original work, citing sources
appropriately, and presenting respectfully

Contest Specifications for this Event
• Contestant may select a topic related to business, entrepreneurship, or Business Professionals of
America, and develop an oral presentation of not less than three (3) or more than five (5) minutes .
• Facts and working data may be secured from any source. This event emphasizes a scholarly approach
to securing information and places emphasis on content and research. Each
contestant’s speech must
be the result of his/her own efforts .
• Speech Outline and Works Cited must adhere to the Style & Reference Manual . Contestants must
place their Contestant ID in the top -right corner of the header on the Works Cited and
Speech
O utline. (This addition is not noted in the Style & Reference Manual .).
• All copies must be printed in black ink on 8 1/2”x11” plain white paper. The copies should be collated
and stapled as separate sets. No binders will be accepted
.
• No materials other than the required submission may be left with judges .

Common Specifications for all BPA Judged Events

• The contestant is responsible for securing a signed BPA Release Form(s) from any person, including
themselves, whose image or work is used in the project .
• The contestant is responsible for citing all sources, including themselves, on the Works Cited page for
any work used on the project. Materials must follow the organization’s BPA Graphic
Standards and
make proper use of the BPA logo and/or organization’s name. Refer to the BPA Graphic Standards in
the Style & Reference Manual .
• It is the policy of Business Professionals of America to comply with copyright laws. The
Style &
Reference Manual contains guidelines for Copyright and/or Fair Use . Contestant(s) will be disqualified
for violations of the guidelines .
• Judges’ comments will be returned digitally through the online judging system at the national level.
Materials submitted for technical judging will not be returned and will not be available at NLC .
