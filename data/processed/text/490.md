## Page 1

490 Arts, Entertainment, & Design Concepts (Open)

(formerly 490 Digital Communication and Design Concepts)

Description & Eligibility
Contestants will assess knowledge of animation, digital media, desktop publishing and web design
languages. Any secondary or postsecondary division contestant may enter this objective event.

Contestant Must Supply
• (Optional) Cordless calculator. Electronic devices will be monitored according to ACT standards.
See Calculator Guidelines. Contestants who violate this rule will be disqualified.

Competition Notes
• Method of evaluation: objective
• AI Policy Level 1 No AI Allowed
• Reference materials are not allowed. Contestants who violate this rule will be disqualified.
• Length of event: no more than sixty (60) minutes testing time
• Unlimited entries by state

Contest Competencies
1.
Apply core visual design principles —including layout, hierarchy, balance, alignment, contrast, and
spacing—to create clear, effective, and audience -appropriate visual communications across digital
media
2. Select and apply typography and color strategically to enhance readability, brand consistency,
accessibility, and visual impact, following industry best practices
3. Integrate visual elements (graphics, shapes, imagery, icons, and motion) effectively to support
messaging, usability, and storytelling objectives
4. Demonstrate ethical and legal use of media assets, including copyright, licensing (Creative
Commons, royalty-free), attribution, and intellectual property considerations
5. Apply animation and motion design principles —including timing, transitions, and visual flow—to
enhance engagement while maintaining clarity and purpose
6. Demonstrate proficiency in video editing techniques, including sequencing, transitions, titles, lower
thirds, and visual effects, aligned to professional production standards
7. Apply audio fundamentals such as sound quality, balance, voice clarity, music selection, and
synchronization to enhance overall media production quality
8. Demonstrate understanding of professional media production environments, including planning,
scripting, production flow, and on-camera or broadcast considerations
9. Apply photography composition techniques (framing, lighting, perspective, rule of thirds, depth) to
capture and select images that strengthen visual storytelling
