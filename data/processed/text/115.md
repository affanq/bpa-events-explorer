115 Advanced College Accounting

Description & Eligibility
Contestants will apply the accounting principles to execute the accounting cycle, record complex
transactions, and prepare and analyze accurate financial statements in compliance with GAAP and
industry standards. Any postsecondary division contestant may e nter this application event.
Contestants may not enter College Accounting and Advanced College Accounting in the same
year.

NLC

Contest Competencies

1.
Execute and articulate each phase in the accounting cycle

2. Prepare, interpret, and analyze interim and end -of-period financial statements and reports
for service, merchandising, and digital business models using accounting platforms.

3. Apply accounting principles to assess and report assets, liabilities, and equity values in
compliance with GAAP
4. Apply periodic and perpetual inventory systems using industry
-standard valuation methods
and software tools
5. Record and explain adjusting entries for accruals, depreciation, prepaids, and deferrals

6. Account for disposal and systematic allocation of long
-term asset costs through
depreciation, depletion, and amortization
7. Apply accounting principles and analyze equity transactions across various business entities

8. Analyze financial statements using ratio analysis and key performance indicators

9. Calculate interest and maturity values of long -term liabilities using financial formulas and
software tools
10. Calculate and record bond transactions using straight -line and effective interest methods in
compliance with GAAP
11. Record merchandise transactions using gross and net methods
in accordance with revenue
recognition standards
