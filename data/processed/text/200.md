Description & Eligibility
Contestants will build essential digital productivity skills for today’s workplace. Learn to create and refine
professional documents and presentations, ensure accessibility, and master spreadsheets and databases
to analyze and visualize data effectively. Any secondary or postsecondary division contestant may enter
this application event.

Contestant Must Supply
• Published and/or unpublished non -electronic written reference materials

Equipment/Supplies Provided

• Computer and software

• Method of evaluation: application

the national level

or keyed) may not be taken into the testing area. Violation of this rule will result in disqualification.

Contest Competencies

1.
Demonstrate proficiency in creating and managing professional business documents using
advanced digital productivity tools and integrated software solutions

2. Create, edit, and proofread professional documents and presentations using modern digital tools,
multimedia elements, and accessibility standards
3. Utilize spreadsheet and database software to organize, analyze, visualize, interpret structured
data, apply formulas, queries, and visualization tools
