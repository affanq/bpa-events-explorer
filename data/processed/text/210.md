Description & Eligibility
Contestants will demonstrate advanced skills to create and format workbooks, import and manage data,
and develop complex formulas, functions, and macros. Gain skills in PivotTables, advanced charts, and
collaborative tools to analyze and present data effec tively. Any secondary or postsecondary division
contestant may enter this application event. Contestants may
not enter Fundamental and Advanced
Spreadsheet Applications in the same year.

Contestant Must Supply
• Published and/or unpublished non -electronic written reference materials

• Computer and software
