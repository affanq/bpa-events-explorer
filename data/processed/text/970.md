Description & Eligibility
Contestants will be given a real -world problem related to a current issue or challenge facing businesses today.
Contestants will d esign a creative and feasible solution and present the solution in a research paper and
presentation. Any middle level contestant may enter this event. Contestants must be registered for the
national competition before the submission deadline and compete in both parts of the event to be ranked.
Entries that do not follow the provided topic, the Copyright and/or Fair Use Guidelines or Artificial
Intelligence Usage & Citation Policy will be disqualified.

Topic
A middle school student in your community has a skill or hobby they love
— whether it's graphic design,
photography, tutoring, pet care, lawn maintenance, or something else entirely. They want to turn that passion
into a real business but aren't sure where to start. They need a plan that is realistic, affordable, and something
a middle schooler could execute.

The Problem
The student has the skill but lacks a business plan. They
don't know how to price their services, find customers,
or manage their money. They've come to you for help building a plan from the ground up.

Read the scenario above and create a complete business plan for a student
-run service business of your
choice. Your research paper must include the following:
• Business Description — What service will the student offer? What makes it unique or valuable?
• Customer Demographics — Who needs this service? Who is the target audience?
• Marketing Strategy — How will the student find and attract clients? Include at least two specific
marketing ideas.
• Pricing and Financial Plan — What will the student charge? What are the startup costs? What is the
projected revenue?
• Challenges and Solutions — What obstacles might this student face and how could they overcome
them?
• Any other information you feel supports your recommendation
