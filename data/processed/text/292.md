292 Project Management Concepts (Open)

Description & Eligibility
Contestants will assess understanding of universal project management concepts and practices aligned
with nationally recognized project management standards, including the Project Management Institute
(PMI) PMBOK® Guide . Any secondary or postsecondary division contestant may enter this objective
event.

Contestant Must Supply
• (Optional) Cordless calculator: Electronic devices will be monitored according to ACT standards.
See NLC Calculator Guidelines .  Contestants who violate this rule will be disqualified
.

Contest Notes
• Method of evaluation: objective
• AI Policy Level 1 No AI Allowed
• Reference materials are not allowed. Contestants who violate this rule will be disqualified.
• Length of event: no more than sixty (60) minutes testing time
• Unlimited entries by state

Contest Competencies

1.
Understand project life cycles, phases, organizational structures, and essential project
management terminology
2. Understand work breakdown structures (WBS) and how they support planning, scheduling, and
task management
3. Analyze project information including tasks, schedules, milestones, deliverables, budgets, and
overall project strategies
4. Understand project risk, quality, and performance concepts, including how uncertainty and
customer expectations impact outcomes
5. Understand leadership, teamwork, communication, stakeholder engagement, and collaboration
tools that support project success
6. Understand ethical behavior, responsibility, and professional conduct in project management
