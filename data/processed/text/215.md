Description & Eligibility
Contestants will design and manage relational databases for accurate data storage and advanced
reporting. Gain skills in creating fields, relationships, and formulas; importing and exporting data; building
queries and filters; and producing user -friendly f orms and professional reports to support data -driven
decisions. Any secondary or postsecondary division contestant may enter this application event.

Contestant Must Supply
• Published and/or unpublished non -electronic written reference materials

Equipment/Supplies Provided

• Computer and software

Contest Notes
• Method of evaluation: application
• AI Policy Level 1 No AI Allowed
• All student-produced jobs completed on -site are uploaded electronically and are not printed at
the national level
• Previous Business Professionals of America tests and/or sample tests (handwritten, photocopied
or keyed) may not be taken into the testing area. Violation of this rule will result in disqualification.

• Length of event: no more than ten (10) minutes orientation, no more than ninety (90) minutes
testing time, and no more than ten (10) minutes wrap -up
• Entries vary by state

Contest Competencies

1.
Design relational databases by defining fields, relationships, and calculated formulas columns to
ensure accurate data storage, retrieval, and advanced reporting capabilities

2. Demonstrate foundational knowledge of database management principles and apply industry
-
standard software to create, modify, maintain, and secure databases
3. Manipulate, import, and export data using queries, filters, and multiple formats to meet
organizational needs
4. Design user-friendly data entry forms and produce professional reports with totals and calculated
fields, analyzing results to identify trends and support decision
-making
