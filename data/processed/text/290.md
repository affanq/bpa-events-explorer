Description & Eligibility
Contestants will demonstrate foundational knowledge of
Management & Entrepreneurship concepts
including business planning, organizational management, marketing, financial fundamentals, and strategic
decision-making. Any secondary or postsecondary division contestant may enter this objective event.

Contest Notes
• Method of evaluation: objective
• AI Policy Level 1 No AI Allowed
• Reference materials are not allowed. Contestants who violate this rule will be disqualified.

• Length of event: no more than sixty (60) minutes testing time
• Unlimited entries by state

Contest Competencies

1.
Demonstrate understanding of entrepreneurship principles, including business plan development,
startup requirements, feasibility analysis, and the characteristics of successful entrepreneurs

2. Demonstrate understanding of management and human resource concepts, including planning,
organizing, leadership, staffing, motivation, and workplace policies
3. Analyze small business strategies by evaluating financial data, marketing plans, operational
decisions, and short - and long-term planning approaches
4. Demonstrate understanding of core marketing concepts, including research, segmentation, value
creation, pricing, and promotion
5. Apply financial literacy skills including interpreting basic financial statements, understanding
startup costs, budgeting, and evaluating business performance metrics
6. Understand legal, ethical, and socially responsible business practices, including regulatory
compliance, business ownership structures, and professional conduct standards

7. Apply strategic decision -making and problem -solving skills to evaluate business challenges and
identify data-driven solutions
8. Understand how management, marketing, HR, finance, and global business concepts connect to
support overall organizational strategy
