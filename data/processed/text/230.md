Description & Eligibility
Contestants will research a workplace -related topic and present their findings in a professional report and
oral presentation. Submissions should demonstrate strong research skills, clear communication, and
evidence-based recommendations. Any secondary div
ision contestant may enter this judged event.
Contestants must be registered for the national competition before the submission deadline and compete
in both parts of the event to be ranked. Entries that do not follow the provided topic or the Copyright
and/or Fair Use Guidelines and Artificial Intelligence Usage & Citation Policy
will be disqualified.

Topic
Research how administrative support professionals are uniquely vulnerable to workplace stress and
burnout due to the high -demand, multi-tasking nature of their roles. Explore the organizational and
personal strategies that can promote wellness, resilience, and long -term career sustainability.

Considerations
• Causes and warning signs of burnout specific to administrative support roles

• Impact of burnout on productivity, accuracy, and overall workplace culture

• Organizational wellness programs, policies, and employer responsibilities
• Ergonomics, physical workspace design, and their effects on employee well
-being
• Mental health resources and support systems available in the workplace

• Skills for setting professional boundaries and managing workload effectively

• Global and cultural considerations in workplace wellness practices

• Recommendations for both employers and administrative professionals to prevent burnout
