Description & Eligibility
Teams will analyze existing and proposed business environments and develop a comprehensive strategy
for implementing a network infrastructure that meets the organization’s needs. This event evaluates the
team’s ability to assess requirements, plan scalable solutions, and present a clear, effective network
design. Any secondary or postsecondary division contestant may enter this judged event.

A team will consist of 2-4 contestants. Teams must be registered for the national competition before the
submission deadline and compete in both parts of the event to be ranked. Entries that do not follow the
provided topic or the Copyright and/or Fair Use Guidelines and the Artificial Intelligence Usage & Citation
Policy will be disqualified.

Topic
Cindershore Esports & Entertainment, headquartered in New York, New York, is a fast -growing
organization that operates three flagship esports arenas inside major entertainment districts in Atlanta,
Georgia; Denver, Colorado; and Phoenix, Arizona. Each arena hosts com petitive gaming tournaments, livestreamed events, and public gaming lounges. Currently, each location relies on a patchwork of ISP -
provided routers, unmanaged switches, and consumer-grade Wi-Fi access points, resulting in high latency
during tournaments, inconsistent streaming quality, frequent network outages during peak events, and no
centralized visibility or control across locations.

As part of their 2027 expansion, Cindershore plans to open three additional arenas in Charlotte, North
Carolina; Sacramento, California; and Minneapolis, Minnesota. These new facilities will be purpose -built,
approximately 8,000 square feet each, and will include a main tournament floor with 64 high-performance
gaming stations, a broadcast studio for live streaming and commentary, a VIP lounge with premium
internet access, administrative offices, and a public café area with complimentary Wi -Fi. Cindershore
Esports & Entertainment needs a proposal for the following:
• High-Throughput, Ultra-Low Latency Network Architecture: Design a network capable of
supporting simultaneous competitive gaming across 64 stations (minimum 25 Gbps backbone
within each facility) with Quality-of-Service (QoS) prioritizing tournament traffic and keeping
round-trip latency under 5ms on local segments.
• Network Segmentation: Create VLANs to isolate tournament gaming stations,
broadcast/streaming equipment, VIP lounge, administrative offices, public café Wi -Fi, and building
management/IoT systems.
• Centralized Management and Monitoring: Propose a unified network management solution (e.g.,
cloud-managed switches/APs or an SD -WAN controller) for all six sites, with real -time
performance dashboards, automated alerting, and remote configuration capabilit ies.
• Security and Remote Support: Incorporate next -generation firewalls, intrusion
detection/prevention systems (IDS/IPS), DDoS mitigation, VPN access for remote administration,
and a role-based access control (RBAC) plan.
• Scalability and Redundancy: Ensure the design can support doubling tournament capacity and
adding satellite pop-up event venues; include redundant ISP uplinks, failover strategies, and high -
availability configurations for core switching.
• Broadcast and Streaming Integration: Plan dedicated network infrastructure supporting HD/4K
live streaming from the broadcast studio, including bonded uplinks, CDN integration
considerations, and stream isolation from public traffic.
• Cost Estimation and ROI Analysis: Provide a detailed bill of materials (switches, routers, APs,
cabling, racks, licensing) and estimate both upfront capital expenditures and ongoing operational
costs, balanced against projected revenue gains from improved tournament reliability and viewer
experience.

Contest Competencies
1.
Design, implement, and optimize network solutions, including routing strategies and VPNs, to meet
organizational requirements for performance, security, and scalability
2. Design and document network cable topologies and create detailed network diagrams using
industry-standard tools to support communication, troubleshooting, and future planning
3. Assess end -user requirements, plan resource placement, evaluate centralized or decentralized
strategies, and incorporate scalability for future expansion in network designs
4. Design and implement redundancy and secure remote access solutions to ensure network
availability and support distributed workforces
5. Evaluate project requirements, delegate responsibilities, and analyze technical support structures
to ensure effective team collaboration and network maintenance
6. Apply analytical and problem -solving techniques to resolve complex networking challenges
and optimize protocols, infrastructure, and host configurations
