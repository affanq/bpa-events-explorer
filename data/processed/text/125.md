125 Payroll Accounting

Description & Eligibility
Contestants will apply payroll principles to calculate earnings, process withholdings, and record
transactions in compliance with GAAP, tax regulations, and current laws using automated systems.
Any
secondary or postsecondary division contestant may enter this application event.

Contestant Must Supply
• Published and/or unpublished non -electronic written reference materials
• Sharpened pencils, pens, ruler
• Cordless calculator: Electronic devices will be monitored according to ACT standards.  See NLC
Calculator Guidelines.  Contestants who violate this rule will be
disqualified.

https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15

Contest Competencies

1.
Apply GAAP and relevant tax principles to calculate, record, and report payroll transactions and
income tax obligations in compliance with current laws and ethical standards
2. Accurately complete onboarding forms and explain statutory and voluntary payroll withholdings
and deductions to ensure compliance and transparency
3. Calculate and verify employee earnings across diverse pay structures, including hourly, salary,
overtime, commission, piece-rate, and blended compensation models
4. Create, maintain, and secure accurate employee earnings records using digital payroll systems,
ensuring data integrity, confidentiality, and compliance with legal requirements
5. Calculate employer payroll tax liabilities and employer -paid benefits in compliance with current
regulations
6. Prepare payroll reports and analyze IRS payroll tax forms, applying federal tax regulations to
ensure compliance with current guidelines
7. Identify and apply current federal, state, and local laws and regulations governing payroll
procedures
8. Record and post payroll transactions in accounting systems to ensure
accurate financial reporting
9. Generate payroll disbursements, prepare tax deposits, and
maintain payroll registers using
automated payroll systems
