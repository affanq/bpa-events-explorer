235 Business Law and Ethics

Description & Eligibility
Contestants will demonstrate knowledge and skills in the areas of ethics, law, business law, and personal
law. Any secondary or postsecondary division contestant may enter this objective event.

Contestant Must Supply
• Published and/or unpublished non -electronic written reference materials

Contest Notes
• Method of evaluation: objective
• AI Policy Level 1 No AI Allowed
• Previous Business Professionals of America tests and/or sample tests (handwritten, photocopied
or keyed) may not be taken into the testing area. Violation of this rule will result in disqualification.

• Length of event: no more than ten (10) minutes orientation, no more than sixty (60) minutes testing
time, no more than ten (10) minutes wrap-up
• Entries vary by state

Contest Competencies

1.
Understand legal terminology, including Latin terms, and office procedures accurately in
documents and communications to ensure efficiency and compliance in a modern legal
environment
2. Analyze and navigate court structures and proceedings while applying ethical standards to ensure
compliance and uphold professional integrity
3. Demonstrate an understanding of self -awareness and effective communication with a diverse
workforce to foster an inclusive and professional legal environment
4. Demonstrate an understanding of sound judgment and decision -making skills to meet or exceed
legal workplace standards and expectations
