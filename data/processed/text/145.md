145 Banking and Finance

Description & Eligibility
Contestants will analyze banking and credit principles, evaluate financial services, and apply secure
practices for transactions, account management, and compliance with regulations. Any secondary or
postsecondary division contestant may enter this applica tion event.

Contestant Must Supply
• Published and/or unpublished non -electronic written reference materials
• Sharpened pencils, pens, ruler
• Cordless calculator: Electronic devices will be monitored according to ACT standards. See
NLC
Calculator Guidelines . Contestants who violate this rule will be disqualified.

• Computer and software

• Method of evaluation: objective | application
• AI Policy Level 1 No AI Allowed

testing area
• Previous Business Professionals of America tests and/or sample tests or facsimiles (handwritten,

Contest Competencies

1.
Analyze factors affecting credit choices, including credit scoring, costs of credit, and cybersecurity,
and describe types of loans and application processes

2. Analyze the functions of commercial paper, secured transactions, and bankruptcy

3. Identify and distinguish the evolving functions and services of traditional banks, online banks, and
financial institutions
4. Develop and implement strategies for secure cash handling and fraud and identity theft prevention

5. Describe the FDIC’s evolving role in the banking industry

6. Describe procedures for electronic banking including mobile apps, biometric authentication, and
real-time payments
7. Define and perform steps to open, maintain, and reconcile checking accounts using traditional and
online platforms
8. Define and calculate both simple and compound interest
9. Describe the advantages and disadvantages of traditional digital savings and investment platforms
in the banking industry
10. Identify government regulations in banking including compliance with digital finance laws and data
protection
11. Explain the fundamentals of cryptocurrency and blockchain technology

12. Describe the custody, storage, and risk of digital assets, and associated security
considerations
13. Evaluate ethical considerations and consumer protection in modern banking

14. Explain the types and functions of digital currencies, including cryptocurrency and
stablecoins
