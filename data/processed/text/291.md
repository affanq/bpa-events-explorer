291 Parliamentary Procedure Concepts (Open)

Description & Eligibility
Contestants will assess knowledge of parliamentary procedure and its role in conducting organized,
democratic, and professional meetings. Competitors will be tested on their understanding of Robert’s
Rules of Order Newly Revised (12th Edition), including m otion classification and processing, debate rules,
voting methods, quorum and agenda requirements, and fundamental meeting management concepts. This
objective test emphasizes accurate application of parliamentary law, ethical meeting practices, and the
principles of fairness, order, and inclusive group decision -making. Any secondary or postsecondary division
contestant may enter this objective event.

Contest Notes
• Method of evaluation: objective
• AI Policy Level 1 No AI Allowed
• Suggested resource: Dunbar’s Manual of Parliamentary Procedure Test Questions

• Reference materials are not allowed. Contestants who violate this rule will be disqualified
• Length of event: no more than sixty (60) minutes testing time
• Unlimited entries by state

Contest Competencies

1.
Understand fundamental parliamentary principles and terminology
2. Identify the classes of motions and their characteristics, including precedence, purpose, and
debate rules
3. Understand the steps and rules for processing motions according to parliamentary procedure

4. Identify voting methods, requirements, and procedures
5. Understand basic meeting structure and management, including the order of business, quorum,
minutes, and officer roles
6. Understand rules of debate and member rights, including limits, decorum, and how members
obtain the floor
7. Identify quorum requirements, minute -taking basics, and the purpose of official meeting records

8. Understand the democratic principles, fairness standards, and ethical expectations that guide
parliamentary procedure
